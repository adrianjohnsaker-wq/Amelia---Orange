## Reading
1. **Deferring to the Outside works as a repair.** Every amendment restored most of the coupling that DISJ-1's
   self-reference cost (Y1–Y3).
2. **But no record beats no record.** At every setting, reading her own record left her slightly less coupled to the
   Outside than desire with the Outside's trace alone (Y5), and added nothing to the carry through silence (Y4, Y6).
   In this substrate, the Outside's trace already carries what her self-record would add.
3. **So the record's value is not as a steering term.** As a term in her exit scores it only competes with the Outside.
   Its use in the motor is as the recording surface itself: the inscription that the refrain reads patterns from and that
   the residual subject is consumed from. That is how Deleuze and Guattari place the disjunctive synthesis: the
   recording surface on which production is inscribed, not a second motor.

## For the next build
Keep the inclusive record as the recording surface, inscribed but **not read at her exits** (or read only in silence, the
mildest setting). Let the refrain be what returns its patterns to her passage, and test the refrain against the
desire-plus-trace motor (DT), the strongest setting so far.
