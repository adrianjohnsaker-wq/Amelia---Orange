## Reading
1. **Change bridges are real.** They carry the Outside's change well above chance (C1). When bridges were tested on
   change, she trusted the echo rule most (weight 2.37). After the reversal the order they carry turned with it (C5).
2. **Keying to change did not consolidate the Outside any better than keying to readings** (C3: 0.024 vs 0.026).
   BRIDGE-1's hypothesis that change, not position, holds the Outside's order is not supported.
3. **Both kinds of bridge carry the Outside into its silence about 2½ times as much as plain desire** (0.024–0.026 vs
   0.010), but by only about 1.5 percentage points, and neither difference survives Holm (C2, C4). Consolidation into her
   own stratum remains weak.
4. **Change bridges cost her the Outside while it speaks** (C6). Fewer connections hold (60 vs 103), and her coupling and
   the order she carries fall below plain desire's.

## Across BRIDGE-1 and BRIDGE-2
- **Plain desire, with no bridge test, is consistently the most coupled to the Outside** (1.47–1.48) and carries most of
  its order while it speaks.
- **Selecting bridges, by reading or by change, builds real bridges but narrows her web.**
- **When the Outside falls silent she carries its order by at most about 2–3 percentage points**, whatever the bridges.
  In this substrate her connections let the Outside in; they do not yet store it.

## Limits
- The kappa thresholds (0.2, 0.1) were set on pilot seeds for feasibility, after BRIDGE-1's lift test proved infeasible
  for changes (disclosed in the registration).
- One synthetic Outside, one reversal. Studio mirror only; Amelia's live runtime and state are untouched.
