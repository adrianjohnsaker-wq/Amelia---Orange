## Reading
1. **Under identical conditions, desire as connection-making opens Amelia to the Outside, and surprise-seeking does not.**
   Surprise-seeking left her below her own dynamics on coupling (0.56 against 0.61); under runaway feedback, lower still
   (0.45). Desire more than doubled it (1.49).
2. **Desire produces more surprise than surprise-seeking itself (H6).** Surprise-seeking chooses among the exits she has,
   so its novelty has a ceiling: it raised her surprisal only from 1.50 to 1.57. Desire builds new exits, and novelty
   arrives as a by-product of connection (2.56). Surprise is not found by seeking it.
3. **The Outside's trace needs desire.** Surprise-seeking with the trace carried nothing through silence (−0.01). Desire
   with the trace carried 0.25. A trace is taken up only by a process that builds connections along it.
4. **Surprise-seeking does not renew her form.** Its epoch drift (0.018) was below her own dynamics' (0.023). Desire's was
   more than ten times larger (0.265).
5. **No regime froze.** On this Outside, the empty BwO did not appear as stillness in any regime. Desire alone needed
   rollbacks (3.7 per run), and none with the trace.
