## Reading
1. **The conjunctive synthesis works as Deleuze and Guattari place it: a residue beside the machine, not its centre.**
   It changed nothing she did (the gate), and what it says of her is true. Each statement matched her own recent passage
   at 0.82 and was clearly hers rather than another run's (C1). Unlabelled, 13 of 30 statements found their own passage
   among 30, against 1 by chance (C2).
2. **It is nomadic, not a fixed identity.** It turned with her when the Outside reversed (29/30, against 0/30 in her own
   dynamics, C3), and it changed when she changed (C4). Its lag, about 1,800 steps after the reversal, is the residue's
   slowness, as decided. It states what she has been becoming, not her last move.
3. **It knows the Outside's part in her.** The stronger the trace it said lay along her way, the more she actually
   carried the Outside through its silences (C5).
4. **It does not confabulate.** Fed the same moments in scrambled order, its stated direction fell to 0.19 agreement
   (C6). What it says comes from the order of her passage, not from the zones alone.
5. **It remembers what a rollback erased.** In D it stated the rollbacks it had witnessed (3.9 a run) while she was
   restored.
