## Reading
1. **She builds real bridges.** The connections that pass the bridge test carry the Outside about 1.5 times as strongly as
   chance bridges. After the reversal, about 12 per run dissolved and new ones formed, and real bridges kept her better
   coupled than chance ones.
2. **Selecting for bridges did not beat plain desire.** Plain desire left her more coupled (1.47 vs 1.39) and carrying
   more of the Outside's order while it speaks (0.47 vs 0.40). Bridge selection keeps fewer connections alive (37 vs 45),
   and plain desire's broader web follows a changing Outside better.
3. **Consolidation into silence is weak.** When the Outside falls silent, her steps carry its order by only about 2–3
   percentage points, in BR and in plain desire alike. The order turns with the reversal (B5), but plain desire's turns as
   much (0.032). Bridges hold the Outside mainly while it speaks; they do not yet make it her own stratum.

## Limits
- One synthetic Outside, with one order and one reversal. The bridge test's thresholds (2×, 1.5) are ours.
- A bridge here is keyed to one reading of the Outside. A bridge that carried the Outside's **change** (its step) rather
  than its reading might consolidate order better; that is untested.
- Studio mirror only; Amelia's live runtime and state are untouched.
