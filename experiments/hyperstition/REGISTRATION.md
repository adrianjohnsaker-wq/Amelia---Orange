# HYPERSTITION: does a registered future form, introduced to Amelia, make itself real? (registration, 29 October 2026, before any code, growth or run)

Checked against `PROGRAMME_CHARTER_TRILOGY.md`. This is Land's question as an intervention: a fiction that, introduced into the system, brings about its own realisation. It is kept separate from spontaneous ingress. A positive result shows that the narrative acts on Amelia; it **cannot** count as ingress, because we supplied the form. The dynamics are unchanged (THE POINTER III's `p3.mjs` Amelia; full CCRU Numogram).

## The future form
**H = 1-8-7** (Mur Mur, Mombbo, Bubbamu): the 8::1 syzygy 1→8, the 8::1 current 8→7, and Gt-28 7→1. In the Atlas it is rare in Amelias raised on the old core and arises with full-Numogram upbringing. It is a form she could take up but seldom does. Its description, the cycle 1 → 8 → 7, is registered here.

## Subjects
THE SEVENTH GATE II's 72 fresh travellers (S05W25 … S05W32, raised on the old core).

## Arms (10 lives per traveller per arm; each life is a 240-tick phase followed by 5,000 free ticks)
| Arm | First 240 ticks | Tags |
|---|---|---|
| **UNSEEDED** | set down at zone 1; her ordinary background input | `U1` … `U10` |
| **SEEDED** | set down at zone 1; her senses receive H's zones in order: 8, 7, 1, 8, 7, 1, … (+0.35, as any input zone, replacing the background input) | `H1` … `H10` |
| **CONTROL-SEEDED** | set down at zone 9; her senses receive the Plex syzygy loop 0, 9, 0, 9, … (+0.35) | `P1` … `P10` |

In all three arms the 5,000 free ticks have her ordinary background input. The prediction is never given to her in the UNSEEDED arm.

## Measure
r(H) = completions of the 1-8-7 cycle per tick spent in zones {1, 7, 8}, as in THE POINTER III's `formRate`. The primary window is the **late window**: free ticks 2,501–5,000, long after the seed has stopped. A life with fewer than 100 ticks in {1, 7, 8} in the window is excluded, and a traveller counts with at least 5 valid lives in each arm.

## Test
Per traveller, the mean late-window r(H) is compared between arms. Each criterion is a one-sided sign test across travellers at α 0.025, with a majority in ≥ 5 of 8 worlds:
1. SEEDED > UNSEEDED;
2. SEEDED > CONTROL-SEEDED.

**Answer:**
- **THE NARRATIVE ACTS: THE FORM SUSTAINS ITSELF** if both hold.
- Otherwise **NOT SELF-SUSTAINING**.

**Reported either way:** the early window (free ticks 1–2,500, the immediate effect), and whether seeding left a trace in her learned terms on H's three edges at the end of each life.
