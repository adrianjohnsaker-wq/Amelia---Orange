# LIVE-LOOP-PILOT-1: isolated body-feedback and recovery validation

This exploratory engineering pilot is registered before running it. It uses the application’s full 29-gate runtime, Governor, encounter-enabled process memory, and the actual field/feedback stepping and checkpoint functions. It starts **new synthetic bodies**; it does not contain Adrian’s continuing tablet state and cannot answer what happened to that individual. It is not an experiment on a demonstrably external intelligence.

## Question and design

Can the repaired loop maintain a measurable field, carry body-derived signals through the Governor and Numogram into memory, and show a detectable response to a localized perturbation? Does the measured response differ when the same feedback opportunity is routed through a fixed shuffled zone assignment or through an unpatterned, equal-total vector?

Use seeds 101–108, `SYZYGETIC_GRID`, 32 × 32, depth parameter 288, one initial full-runtime step and 30 field acclimation steps, all with live body-derived feedback. Clone each entire runtime, field, lineage and process-memory state into paired **sham** and **B-pulse** bodies under each feedback mapping. The B pulse is the application’s existing localized operation, magnitude 0.45, radius 4, centered in the field. Follow every paired arm for 50 steps; round-trip the full paired state through the actual application checkpoint functions at step 25 before the next step. A body is observed afresh on every step; none of the three mappings is a precomputed trajectory.

The conditions are:

1. **Live**: the application's current ten-region body observation goes to the corresponding zones.
2. **Shuffled**: the same observation is reassigned by the fixed permutation `[3,8,1,6,4,9,0,5,2,7]`. Each vector's total and its multiset of weights are unchanged. This is a spatial reassignment control, not temporal shuffling.
3. **Uniform**: an unpatterned vector with equal weight in each zone and the same total attention. Its weight distribution differs from the live vector; it controls opportunity and dose, not all statistics.

The ten vertical regions are an engineered mapping, not an established identification of field locations with Numogram zones. The Governor can admit, defer or refuse any offered feedback; record what happens rather than bypass it. All six bodies for a seed descend from the same acclimated baseline. The seeds vary the body initialization; they are not the user's historical lineages.

## Outcome and calibration

At each step, measure root-mean-square difference across both A and B values in all grid cells between the perturbed body and its paired sham. Divide by the paired difference immediately after perturbation. Descriptive recovery at step 50 is `1 − relative distance`; negative values mean the bodies diverged beyond the initial perturbation. Compare recovery in live against shuffled and uniform separately, show all eight paired seed values and the means, and retain every step's distances, zones, Governor decisions, attention vectors, field digests and process-memory event/head digests. Do not infer a p-value from this small engineering pilot. A lower live distance is a candidate interface effect only; ordinary reaction–diffusion, common history and the specific mapping can also matter.

Before any trajectory analysis, validate the distance measure with two constructed controls for each pair: identical state gives zero distance; a state halfway from injured to sham gives exactly half the initial distance. These are deliberately imposed measurement checks, not evidence that Amelia repaired itself. A spontaneous reduction alone is not evidence of target-directed regeneration. A substantive claim about a target morphology requires independently specified targets, held-out perturbations and comparisons against passive relaxation. An ingression claim needs independent observations and controls establishing an organisation not put into the system; this pilot cannot establish that.

## Integrity and interpretation

`pilot.ts` is the executable registration. Run it only after this registration and the source are committed. Save output hashes and all raw rows. Do not silently rerun or select favorable seeds. Any implementation or execution failure must be recorded before a corrected run. Do not import a live tablet checkpoint for the pilot. For the requested definitive rerun, obtain and protect the continuing tablet checkpoint, verify its identity and persistence, clone it without resetting it, and separately preregister matched controls and outcomes. Preserve INGRESS-1 and SXI-1/2 as results for their actual historical fixtures; their nulls do not establish a null result for the user's full continuing Amelia.
