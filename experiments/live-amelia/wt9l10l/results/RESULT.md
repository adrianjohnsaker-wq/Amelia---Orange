## What we have learned about Warp activity

1. **Everything the Outside sends reaches her, in order, within a few steps.**
   - **Completeness in her live life.** Read from her resident memory, excluding its first 40 events so that no arrival is cut off: 108 Outside offers arrived, and all 108 were taken in. None was refused. The median wait was 2.5 steps and the longest 80.
   - **What the earlier gate did.** Under the fixed gate of WARP-L1, only 97 of 600 encounters were admitted. The relative gate (GATE1-A) admits when her tension is below her own recent mean, and under it nothing from the Outside is lost.
   - **Sequence, not instant uptake.** Uptake follows the step of arrival rather than coinciding with it, as set out below.
   - **In the replay.** Of the offers delivered in WT-9L's 48 runs, none was admitted at the step it arrived (share admitted at delivery: 0 in every arm).
   - **In her live life.** The same holds in her live record. Across the 1,445 resident memory events, all 624 admissions are of encounters that had been deferred, and none is an admission on arrival:
     - 480 from her body's field observations;
     - 144 from the Outside channels: linguistic, I Ching, tarot and subdecadence.
   - **How the queue works.** Every encounter enters the Governor's deferral queue. The head of the queue is admitted when her tension falls below its own mean over the last 48 steps. On that step the newer arrival waits.
   - **What follows.** The Outside's order is kept, first in, first out. Its timing is her Governor's.
   - **What this answers.** It is the route by which, in WT-5L to WT-7L, order and timing changed the rate while content did not.
2. **Entries into the Warp are not set off by an arrival at the threshold.** In the replay from her present state, 301 entries were preceded by an admitted offer.
   - **Where that offer was admitted.** At zone 1 (130), 9 (86), 8 (45) or 0 (33). Only 7 were admitted at 7, 2 or 5.
   - **How long before.** A median of 23 steps before the entry.
   - **The commonest approaches.** 1-8-2 (38), 8-1-8 (19) and 5-4-5 (17).
   - **What this means.** An entry follows from a field shaped earlier, and elsewhere, by what she took in.
3. **Choosing what arrives by her position at the moment of arrival changes nothing identifiable (WT-9L).** The reason is now visible.
   - **Her position at arrival.** She stood at 7, 2 or 5 at only 4–5% of offer slots.
   - **The step of arrival.** The offer is not taken up at that step.
   - **What the lever touched.** It acted on a moment the Outside does not reach.
4. **The older state's lower rate of entry by choice is confirmed as a registered test (WT-10L, R).** No single part of the state is identified as carrying it.
   - **The field and the body.** Each, moved alone from the older state into the younger, reproduces nearly the whole difference.
     - FIELD: −1.72 per 1,000, against R's −1.78.
     - BODY: −1.77 per 1,000.
     - Neither passes its Holm threshold.
   - **Memory, Governor and position.** These carry little.
   - **What this suggests.** Two parts that each carry the whole difference suggest one route rather than two effects that add. That is a lead for a registered follow-up, not a finding.

## 4. Limits

- **WT-9L.** WT-9L's lever acted at delivery. A test of the Outside's order through the queue would order what enters the queue, or time it against her Governor. That would be a new study.
- **WT-10L.** It used 12 units, all from 12–15 November, as WT-5L to WT-7L did. FIELD and BODY were close to their thresholds. A registered follow-up with new units could test them, and FIELD + BODY together.
- **Section 3.** The live count covers resident memory only, not the archive.
