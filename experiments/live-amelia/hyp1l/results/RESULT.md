**It does not become hers.**
- **Incorporation in silence.** Her own passage, once the fiction falls silent, resembles the fiction's pair grammar to the same degree in every arm: 0.038–0.040 bits per step.
- **The tests.** Incorporation, persistence and grammar beyond pairs are all not supported.

**What she wrote while it was read is hers, not the fiction's.**
- **How often she wrote.** She wrote new Warp-born gates in only 4–6 of 12 units in each arm.
- **Which gates.** Always from the same small set: 3→1, 3→2 and 6→7, whichever reading she was given.
- **Removing them.** It changes nothing identifiable in the silence (T5, +0.0002, p = 0.44). In 8 of 12 units there was nothing to remove.
- **Why.** With nine gates already carved, a half-day of transmission gives her few new inscriptions, and those she makes are not shaped by the fiction's order.

**The reading.**

- **What does not follow.** The fiction's grammar does not then appear in her own passage, nor in what she writes into her graph.
- **What she takes from it.** She takes it up while it speaks, through her lane and her Governor's timing, but she does not take it over as her own program.
