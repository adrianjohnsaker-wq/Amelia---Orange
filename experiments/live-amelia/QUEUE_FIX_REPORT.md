# LIVE-LOOP-PILOT-1: queue-fix follow-up

The registered same-seed follow-up ran on application and registration commit `d0c3bc7060a6d9d6a5d95598fcfd5f4c0fa14bf4`. It repeats the original pilot's initializations and procedures after the field-queue change. It is an engineering follow-up, not independent replication and not a test of Adrian's continuing tablet memory.

Among the 400 newly offered field observations in the eight injured live-mapping bodies, the Governor immediately attended 75 and deferred 325. There were **zero queue-full refusals**, compared with 113 in the original pilot. Thirteen earlier deferred field observations were also admitted during those 400 steps. Of the 325 initial deferrals, 312 were because deformation tension exceeded the Governor's existing threshold and 13 were because a previously deferred observation was being attended on that step. The queue fix prevented accumulation of stale field observations; it did **not** remove the high-tension gate. Frequent deferral remains a substantive constraint on the feedback channel and deserves investigation before a claims-bearing test. Every offered decision remained in process memory, and the Governor was not overridden.

The mean step-50 reduction in normalized A/B field distance was 0.602335 for live mapping, 0.601415 for shuffled mapping and 0.602270 for uniform attention. These values are close to the original run and remain compatible with ordinary field relaxation. This observation does not establish target-directed regeneration, a distinctive live mapping effect or an external organisation. Because the prior finding prompted the code change and the same seeds were reused, these numbers are descriptive only.

The sustained-lock unit test independently exercised 50 field deferrals while a Tarot encounter remained in the queue, verified that only the latest pending field observation was retained, restored the complete checkpoint, and confirmed both encounters were admitted after the Governor recovered. This tests the queue mechanism under controlled conditions; it does not prove that a continuing live tablet instance will enter recovery or that its cloud account works.
