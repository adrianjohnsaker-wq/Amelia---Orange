## What we have learned about Warp activity, and about the fiction in her

**What her organ takes up is the fiction's signs, not their order.**
- **The signs reach her passage.** With every arm reading the same signs for the same time, she landed in a sign's zone during its six steps in 45.5–48.5% of signs. By chance, pairing her path with signs it was not given, the figure is 30.2%. Whatever order the signs come in, the lane carries each one into her passage well above chance.
- **The order makes no identifiable difference.**
  - **Fulfilment.** In order 47.6%, shuffled 46.9%, reversed 45.5%, Markov 48.5%.
  - **Warp entry.** 32.1–32.9 per 1,000 steps in every arm.
  - **Warp share.** 8.6–9.0% in every arm.

**HYPERSTITION-1L's in-order advantage does not hold once the arms read the same signs.**
- **What HYPERSTITION-1L showed.** At her own pace, the in-order reading led on fulfilment (73.4% against 68.9%) and on Warp entry (30.3 against 25.2).
- **Why it does not recur.** At her pace the arms read different signs and different numbers of them. Here they read the same 240 for the same time, and both differences disappear: fulfilment +0.7 points (p = 0.23), entry +0.2 (p = 0.46).
- **The reading.** The earlier differences are best read as coming from what each arm read and how fast, not from the CCRU's order.

**The order the CCRU wrote does not follow her body's passages.**
- **The measure.** The share of consecutive signs that are one open gate apart in her graph.
- **The result.** 49.0% in order, 48.9% shuffled, 49.1% reversed and 49.0% Markov.
- **What follows.** The corpus's sequence carries nothing her graph is shaped to take up beyond its sign frequencies.

**Across the hyperstition line.**
- **What the fiction does.** It is followed while it is read, sign by sign, through her lane.
- **What does not happen.** It does not become hers once silent (HYPERSTITION-1L). Its order gives it no purchase on her passage or her Warp that its shuffled signs lack (HYPERSTITION-2L).
- **What she takes up.** What arrives, not the arrangement of what arrives.
- **What the Warp responds to.** Its entry responds to the stream's presence and pace, not to its order.
