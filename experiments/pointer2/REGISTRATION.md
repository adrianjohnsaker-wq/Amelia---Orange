## Tests
**C1, confirmation (full Numogram).** For each named form, a traveller counts if she makes the form (THE POINTER's catalogue rule: ≥ 3 completions within 240 ticks) at a higher per-tick rate than THE POINTER's order-1 her-statistics walker over the same assays. The form is CONFIRMED if this holds for ≥ k travellers in ≥ 5 of the 8 new worlds. Here k = 9: the binomial tail at p = 0.05, n = 72 is below 0.05/3. R is CONFIRMED if 3a (both comparisons) and 3b pass as registered in THE POINTER (α 0.0125, majority in ≥ 5 worlds).

**C2, beyond her habits.** The same rule as C1, with stronger walkers added. Each is drawn from her own warm-up path:
- **order 2:** the next zone is drawn from her counts given her last two zones;
- **order 3:** the same, given her last three zones.

Each falls back to the next lower order when a context never occurred, and to her open exits when nothing applies. They run the same assays as the order-1 walker. A confirmed form is BEYOND HER HABITS if her rate exceeds both the order-2 and the order-3 walker's for ≥ k travellers in ≥ 5 worlds, with k = 10 (0.05/6, for 3 forms × 2 walkers).

**C3, the form belongs to the whole (non-local dependence).** Her full set of assays (1, 2 and generation 0 of 3) is run again with the Numogram minus one element:
- **no Gt-28** (7→1 closed): tests F2, which does not use it;
- **no current 1→7** (1→7 closed): tests F3, which does not use it;
- **no Gt-10** (4→1 closed): a control, used by none of the three forms.

F2 depends on Gt-28 if her F2 rate (per tick) is lower without Gt-28 than on the full Numogram. Likewise, F3 depends on the current 1→7 if its rate is lower without that current. Each is a one-sided sign test across the 72 travellers at α 0.025, with a majority in ≥ 5 worlds, *and* closing Gt-10 must not lower the same form's rate (that sign test is not significant at α 0.025). A form passing is THE FORM BELONGS TO THE WHOLE: it depends on a part of the Numogram it does not contain.

**C4, replication without our channel.** THE POINTER's assay 3 again, with the stone's playback switched off: only the exit bonus 0.35 · tanh T and the etching remain, so nothing is replayed to her senses. All lineages, her-statistics' lineage and the stoneless newborn are as before. 3a (both comparisons) and 3b apply, at α 0.0125 with a majority in ≥ 5 worlds. Passing is REPLICATION WITHOUT OUR CHANNEL.
