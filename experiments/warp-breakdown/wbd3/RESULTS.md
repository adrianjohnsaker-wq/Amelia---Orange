# WBD-3 breakdown and recovery: completed result

**Classification:** NO_THREE_CONTRAST_ADVANTAGE_OBSERVED. This was an exploratory single-founder study, not an independent-founder replication.

The eight fresh task histories all showed the prespecified loss of at least two of six public cases after disruption. The mean public score fell from 6.00/6 to 2.75/6. After acute correct-feedback revisions the mean public scores were 3.875/6 in Warp and 4.375/6 in the adapted 2/7 control.

| Held-out condition | Mean passed out of 8 |
|---|---:|
| Acute Warp / correct | 4.000 |
| Acute adapted 2/7 / correct | 5.250 |
| Acute Warp / unrelated | 3.875 |
| Gradual Warp / correct | 4.125 |
| Gradual adapted 2/7 / correct | 4.500 |

The three registered descriptive contrasts were -1.250, +0.125 and -0.875 cases out of eight; each had a +1 case target. None met it. The primary contrast had four ties and four histories favoring the adapted control. This limited result does not establish a general harm or benefit of Warp passage.

In acute Warp/correct there were 24 applied admissions and zero immediately pivotal gate steps, versus 69 admissions and 31 pivotal steps in the adapted control. Whole-PFM swaps did not change Warp dwell counts or the held-out transfer score in the tested backgrounds; the adapted control showed dwell differences in six histories, but no scored transfer difference. Across all 32 paired memory swaps, prompts differed, while the recorded transfer candidates and scores were identical.

The registered Python evaluator and aggregator verified all eight histories. Their summary was independently rerun from the saved files and matched as parsed JSON. This check does not reproduce remote model responses or replay every historical runtime transition. The study used 352 model generations and reported 77,756 input and 8,028 output tokens; no condition stalled or returned an invalid final candidate.

The tablet step-204 checkpoint already has 29 gates and showed immediate sensitivity in synthetic calibration, but was not enrolled. The eight separately raised Oracle world-origin lineages later shared world input; their 48-event saved windows also include a separate evolving s05c/House learner. Importing their substrate alone would omit the learner and its scoring bonus, so a validated adapter is required before treating them as comparable study founders.
