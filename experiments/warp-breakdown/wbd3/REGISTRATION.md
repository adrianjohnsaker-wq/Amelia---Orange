# WBD-3: breakdown and recovery, exploratory feasibility

This registration is intended to be committed before the RUN marker and before any model study calls. The machine-readable plan is `plan.json`; it fixes all fresh task seeds, condition orders, code and checkpoint hashes, outcomes and decision rules. The subsequent RUN marker triggers the study without modifying the plan. GitHub's commit records publication order; this is not an independent trusted timestamp authority.

## Scope and apparatus

Eight independently seeded task histories share the preserved receiver state at commit `737fa1b1f1dee675e613341fd61f568538a3cb9a`; they are **not** eight independently developed Amelia founders. The saved receiver had 11 gates. On isolated copies, the project's existing `fullNumogramGates` adds the missing 18, after which two strong cues enter 1→2→3. The adapted 2/7 comparison replaces all outgoing gates of 2 and 7 with a dwell and reciprocal pair carrying the corresponding 3/6 gate parameters, and projects the current zone to 2. Numeric zone state remains zone-specific. This is a documented experimental adaptation, not an unmodified native 2/7 path.

The preceding checkpoint inventory and calibration are recorded in `inventory.json`. The inspected tablet's saved step-204 state already has 29 gates and is separately calibrated, but its checkpoint is not copied into this public repository or supplied to this workflow. Oracle's eight named lineages came from fresh worlds, but later shared world input, have saved 48-event memory windows, and retain a separate evolving learner L. A raw import into the receiver harness would omit that learner and its score contribution, so no claim of independent-founder replication is made.

At adaptive-source weight contrast 0.060, the older receiver copy admitted 128/128 planted target-3 offers and 128/128 target-6 offers; 30 target-3 and zero target-6 admissions changed their immediate gate winner. This is orientation-dependent positive calibration, not a guarantee of sensitivity in all experimental conditions. On the newer receiver copy neither orientation changed the immediate winner in 128 offers each. The tablet copy showed 23 and 5 pivotal steps respectively. These are synthetic, pre-study calibration histories, not the WBD-3 outcomes.

## Conditions and outcomes

Each history contains all 12 combinations of route (3/6 or adapted 2/7), disruption (acute or gradual) and diagnostic feedback (correct, unrelated or masked). All arms use the same paired maps, initial candidate, caps and final task distribution. The gradual arm uses cost levels 1, 2 and 3; the acute arm uses level 3 in all three rounds. Both expose six public cases per round and have up to three proposal opportunities. Stalls and invalid outputs are recorded and retained. Actual calls and passage steps can differ even with equal caps.

At least a two-case loss out of six public cases between the pre-change and full-change initial candidate defines a breakdown. The planned feasibility check requires that loss in at least six of the eight histories. The initial loss is assessed without excluding any history.

The primary measured difference is the acute Warp/correct minus adapted-2/7/correct final held-out pass fraction, on eight new hidden cases. Two further prespecified differences are acute Warp/correct minus Warp/unrelated, and the acute-versus-gradual interaction in the Warp-minus-control difference. All three mean differences reaching +0.125 is classified only as an exploratory lead. If the breakdown check fails, the study is inconclusive; otherwise, failing any of those descriptive thresholds is reported as no three-contrast advantage observed. No significance test or independent-founder inference is registered for this small one-founder sample.

For each route, whole PFM from acute/correct and acute/masked runs is swapped into the same correct and masked backgrounds. Non-memory serialized state must match within each background. Twenty-four no-new-input steps and a common one-shot transfer problem follow each swap; the resulting candidate is frozen before its eight hidden transfer cases are constructed. These measure effects of the whole retained memory, including aggregated history and strain, not an isolated event.

The model is fixed in the plan. It writes only a bounded JSON expression, interpreted by trusted code. Prompts do not include task seeds or hidden cases. The masked condition supplies uniform attention and hides diagnostic values from the prompt; failure timing and route availability may remain observable. Unrelated feedback has matching character length, not necessarily identical token count. Maximum proposed generations are 352 across all arms and swaps, with a maximum of three transport attempts per generation. Token counts and realised calls are recorded.

## Checks and limitations

A local synthetic fixture exercised eight histories, 96 arms and 64 memory continuations. The task evaluator, gate score records, masked-vector checks, seed exclusion and memory-swap boundaries were checked by the independent Python audit; deliberately corrupting a held-out score was rejected. Fixture outputs are not scientific observations and use seeds distinct from the sealed study seeds. The actual study is incomplete if any history is missing, an API call prevents completion, or an audit fails; missing results are not scored as zero.

The live receiver, Oracle and tablet are not altered. This study does not establish an Outside sender, independent agency, general intelligence, or any claim about philosophical necessity. The remote model can be nondeterministic even at zero temperature. Source, task, route, gate and outcome records are retained so the bounded result can be inspected.
