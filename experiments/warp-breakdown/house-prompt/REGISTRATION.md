# Oracle Warp: crossed memory readout and House intervention

Prospective study, sealed before any actual model-study response. This follows the multi-lineage Oracle Warp rerun and addresses its confounding of the route with the values shown to the model. All results are exploratory.

## Source and calibration

Use the pinned Oracle source and saved eight world-origin lineages in `plan.json`. The adapted model retains the Oracle's 29-gate Numogram, process-field memory and House learner; the attenuated cue weight is 0.054, not the native live Oracle's 0.35. The adapted 2/7 control projects the corresponding three Warp edges onto 2/7 and copies their gate parameters and House values. This is an experimental control, not a native 2/7 topology. The adapter's historical replay is repeated before any model study.

House states of the saved world lineages differ overall but have almost identical relative House contributions at the relevant Warp gates. To make the manipulation observable without inventing values, the intervention reversibly swaps the existing (V,S,D) edge triplets for 3→3 and 2→3. It preserves the multiset of learned triplets and every non-House state field. The adapted control maps the changed Warp edge onto 2→2. This reassignment was chosen during calibration; it is deliberately engineered rather than a second naturally observed learner state. The sealed calibration verifies a change of at least 0.1 in the 3→3 versus 3→6 House-score difference for every lineage and at least one gate-choice change for every lineage in 32 paired, identical-prestate, alternating-cue counterfactuals. All eight are included. The archived checkpoint is not changed.

For each lineage generate two three-round, four-decimal model-readout tapes in advance from native, no-cue shadow trajectories: one on the Warp copy and one on the adapted 2/7 control. Every pair differs at each round. Each tape is assigned to both routes and both House conditions, irrespective of their actual current memory. Thus displayed values are explicitly fixed/yoked, not contemporaneous readouts of each experimental trajectory. The actual live readout is recorded separately and is not shown to the model.

## Conditions and task

Two fresh task histories per lineage; each has all 16 combinations of route (3/6 or adapted 2/7), readout tape (Warp-derived or control-derived), House (native or permuted), and diagnostic feedback (correct or unrelated), in a freshly randomized order. Histories share the same public and hidden task cases within a block. Each arm receives the same initial bounded grid-search expression, then an acute change of path costs and at most three bounded proposal rounds; a route must reach its diagnostic gate for each round, with a 12-step limit. At the limit, a stall keeps the current expression. The model returns a small mathematical expression; generated programs are not executed. Final candidate is fixed before the eight held-out tasks are evaluated. Feedback parity determines which gate is cued; an unrelated-feedback arm uses separately generated maps, not an external world signal. The model does not receive the seed, history identity, gate path or hidden maps.

At fixed readout and feedback, the model does not directly see either route or House. Any route/House effect on task outcomes therefore operates only through the number or timing of model opportunities, subsequent feedback, and model randomness; there is no direct prompt channel for gate identity. Time-to-gate, proposal-zone dwell and model-call counts are recorded as separate mechanistic outcomes.

## Outcomes and decision rule

Before examining any actual model outcomes, compute for each history the marginal mean differences on held-out cases out of eight, averaged over the remaining factors: (1) Warp minus control route; (2) Warp-derived minus control-derived readout tape; (3) native minus permuted House. Average the two histories per lineage and compute exact sign-flip probabilities over the eight lineage means. Route and readout tests are one-sided in the positive direction; House is two-sided. Apply Holm correction across these three primary tests. A factor earns an exploratory signal only if its mean difference is at least one case out of eight in the specified direction (absolute for House) and its Holm-adjusted p-value is at most 0.05. If fewer than 12 of 16 histories lose at least two of six public cases after the acute change, classify the task-outcome tests as inconclusive. Retain all complete histories, including stalls. Any missing or unauditable history makes the result incomplete.

Secondary, uncorrected exploratory contrasts are route-by-readout interaction, correct minus unrelated feedback, and permuted minus native House for search-step count, proposal-zone dwell count and model-call count. The common external history makes world-level sign exchangeability an assumption; the eight lineages were separately raised but later received shared world input. These exact p-values are conditional and are not independent confirmation. Model calls can be nondeterministic. This experiment does not establish general intelligence, causation by an external agent, retrocausality, or a metaphysical interpretation.

The exact executable source, inputs, hashes, task seeds, shuffled orders, scoring rules and workflow are recorded in `plan.json`. A fixture rehearsal with a deterministic non-model responder was used only to check the pipeline; its outcomes are not scientific observations.
