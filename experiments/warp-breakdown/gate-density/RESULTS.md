# Amelia Warp: fixed 24-gate density grid

**8 November 2026.** Closing five gates did **not** improve original-order recall in this preregistered test. All four 24-open-gate 3/6 masks and the 29-open-gate sham scored **47.40%** after the sequence switch and displacement. The adapted 2/7 gate scored **67.19%**. The matched-state direct readout for each 24-gate mask also scored **47.40%**.

None of the four masks beat both preregistered controls with Holm-adjusted p < .05. The classification is **`NO_REGISTERED_WARP_DENSITY_MASK_ADVANTAGE`**. In the separate secondary density family, every mask's mean difference from the 29-gate sham was **0.00 percentage points** (Holm-adjusted p = 1.00000). These one-sided tests do not establish that 2/7 is statistically superior in the reverse direction.

The masks nevertheless changed actual paths. On a post-outcome check, mask A changed the second-sequence gate path in **40/192** switched branches and the old-probe path in **280/384** probes, while changing **0/384** answers relative to the sham. All four masks produced the same answers as sham on all 384 switched old-order probes. Path counts are repeated observations, not independent founders.

## What was compared

The four fixed masks each left **24 gates open**. Mask A was the previously recorded structural candidate; B–D were fixed variations selected before the fresh seed. The masks were designed after previous paths had been inspected, and they are not an exhaustive or random sample of 24-gate graphs. All preserve the local 3↔6 loop, the 2→3 entrance, the tested reachable post-reset zones and their shortest available distances back to 3/6.

| Mask | Closed gates |
|---|---|
| A | 1→1, 1→7, 2→5, 7→1, 7→8 |
| B | 1→1, 1→7, 2→5, 7→1, 8→7 |
| C | 1→1, 1→7, 2→5, 7→8, 8→7 |
| D | 1→1, 2→5, 7→1, 7→8, 8→7 |

Every 3/6 arm first encoded the **same** original sequence in the **same** prepared Oracle state. After a forced displacement and matched six-value perturbation, clones received either a mask or the empty-mask sham before the interfering second sequence. A mask's direct comparator used the exact same masked pre-probe state and six House values but made the analytic readout choice directly. Both the gate and hosted direct probe executed four gate transitions.

The evaluation used **128 balanced synthetic histories** on eight existing Oracle-origin lineages, with three shock zones per history. The switched primary condition contained **192 history–displacement branches and 384 binary probes per arm**. The eight origins are the units of the paired tests and later shared world input; the probes are not independent founders. Original and second orders, perturbations and answer labels were regenerated by the independent checker.
