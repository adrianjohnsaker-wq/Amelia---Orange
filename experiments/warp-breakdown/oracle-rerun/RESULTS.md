# Oracle Warp multi-lineage rerun: completed result

**Classification:** NO_THREE_CONTRAST_ADVANTAGE_OBSERVED. Sixteen task histories were run, two for each of eight separately raised world-origin lineages. The lineages later shared world input; this is exploratory.

The House-preserving adapter reproduced 43 historical Oracle exchanges and 129 transitions, with 7,724 numerical fields matching across the eight final saved states after excluding epoch snapshot timestamps. The Oracle's usual truncated save produced two documented telemetry-counter differences in a separate resume check. All eight lineages were calibrated before the study.

The task change met the breakdown criterion in 15/16 histories: public performance fell from 6.00/6 to 2.5625/6 on average. Acute Warp/correct recovered to 4.375/6 public and 5.125/8 held-out cases; adapted 2/7/correct recovered to 3.625/6 public and 4.4375/8 held-out.

| Prespecified contrast | Mean difference, cases out of 8 | Conditional one-sided world sign-flip p | Practical target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Acute Warp/correct minus adapted 2/7/correct | +0.6875 | 0.03125 | >= +1 |
| Acute Warp/correct minus Warp/unrelated | +0.0625 | 0.50000 | >= +1 |
| Acute route difference minus gradual route difference | +0.3750 | 0.12500 | >= +1 |

Five world-origin lineage averages favoured Warp on the primary contrast and three were tied. The primary p-value assumes conditional lineage-level sign exchangeability and tests a zero-difference null; it does not establish the presealed +1 case target. The joint decision required all three practical and probability thresholds, so it was not met.

Immediate gate choice changed on 2 of 55 acute Warp/correct coded steps and 0 of 48 corresponding control steps. Whole-House swaps produced no measured dwell or held-out transfer differences; one PFM swap changed a Warp dwell count, and none changed the transfer score. Post-run donor checks found House and PFM donors identical in 14/16 Warp histories and all 16 controls, limiting the informativeness of most swaps.

A post-run prompt check found that all 16 initial acute Warp/correct and control/correct prompt pairs differed only in the displayed local memory values. First candidates differed in 12 pairs and final candidates in 9. The primary contrast is therefore a coupled route-and-model-prompt result; it does not isolate a causal effect of 3/6 passage or the House learner.

The experimental +0.054 coded-zone bonus was selected from synthetic calibration before task histories to match WBD-3's gate-relevant score contrast; the live Oracle's native weight is +0.35. This input uses the Oracle's native scoring mechanism and not the separate Outside receiver's Governor admission queue. The adapted 2/7 control changes the graph and three mapped House entries.

All 16 histories and the sealed audit completed, with 832 model generations and no stalls or invalid final candidates. The saved Python audit and aggregator were rerun locally; their regenerated summary matched the published summary as parsed JSON. This checks recorded outcomes and consistency, not the historical model calls or every runtime transition.
