## Mechanism

The present field is observed afresh at every step as mean B in ten vertical bands, normalized to an attention vector. These bands are an engineered mapping to the Numogram zones. The synthetic source computes a current body centroid from that vector, updates its own persistent exponential memory (`0.65 × previous + 0.35 × current`), selects address A below 0.5 or B otherwise, and responds with the associated zone profile. A is `[0.235, 0.185, 0.145, 0.115, 0.095, 0.075, 0.055, 0.045, 0.030, 0.020]`; B is its reverse. The profiles are intentionally planted in an engineered adaptive source. They are fixed target profiles, **not a precomputed schedule**: the selected address and response are recomputed from the current body and source memory at each step.

The application checks that the source offer is bound to the exact current field digest, and records an offer digest over the source state, body digest and response. The existing Governor then decides whether to attend, defer or refuse; the full Numogram can select only its existing open exits; PFM records the decisions and feeds the existing field step. The source's response occupies the existing `SYNTHETIC_FIELD` feedback slot during exposure, rather than adding an ungated parallel input. The research trace links each offered source response to its body digest, decision, Numogram transition, PFM event and resulting field. There is one submitted feedback event per step in every arm.

## Conditions

Eight new fields use seeds 101–108, size 32 × 32, `SYZYGETIC_GRID`, depth parameter 256; each is attached to a fresh clone of the same synced runtime. Each has 20 acclimation steps of ordinary body feedback. At that point, four copies of that particular body/runtime are compared:

- **Live source:** the adaptive source responds to its own current body and offers the A/B profile.
- **Shuffled source:** the same adaptive mechanism responds to its own body, but its output is reassigned using `[3,8,1,6,4,9,0,5,2,7]`. On every step, its response has the same multiset of weights and total as its own unshuffled profile. This is a zone-assignment shuffle, not a temporal shuffle.
- **Uniform source:** the same source observes its own body, but the offered vector is uniform 0.1 per zone. Total weight is matched; its weight distribution is not.
- **Ordinary feedback:** no source is active; the application's normal current body vector is offered. Its total is normalized but its distribution is not matched to the source profile.

The arms start from the identical acclimated checkpoint for each seed. They remain subject to the same unmodified Governor thresholds and one-offer-per-step pathway. Since bodies and source states can diverge, the shuffled and live arms are not guaranteed identical vectors after the split. None of the conditions is an independent continuation of the user's tablet history.

Each arm has 40 source-exposure steps followed by 30 steps in which no *new* source response is offered and ordinary field feedback resumes. At exposure step 21 the existing field cells are mirrored horizontally in every arm, rearranging the same cell values instead of assigning an address by hand. At overall step 51 the existing application applies a centered B pulse with magnitude 0.45 and radius 4 in every arm. At steps 20 and 55 the body, runtime, PFM, Governor and source state are serialized, saved and restored into a newly constructed runtime before continuing. These are serialized application-runtime restarts in the experiment driver; they are not a tablet app restart or a real Supabase synchronization test.

At source withdrawal, pending source encounters can still be in the Governor's queue. Record pending counts and any later admissions rather than treating cessation of new offers as proof that all earlier source effects have disappeared. PFM's encounter contribution remains in the normal recent-memory window by design.

## Outcomes and controls

The runner preserves a step-level record of body observations and hashes, source state and offered vector, the current and previously queued Governor decisions, all open outgoing gates, selected zone, PFM event/head and bias, resulting field observation, and interventions. Full serialized checkpoint copies remain in the private study evidence; public traces must exclude the raw user archive and account identifiers.

Primary exploratory readouts:

1. **Address response to body rearrangement:** in each source arm, whether the selected address during the five steps after mirroring differs from the address at step 20. Report the actual centroid before and after, without assuming the mirror must switch the address.
2. **Admission and routing:** count current offers attended, deferred and refused; separately count any source offers admitted later. Record exact selected Numogram transitions and whether the source vector was actually applied. An episode with fewer than five immediately attended source offers during the 40-step exposure is marked insufficient for a source-effect interpretation. This is an operational minimum, not a statistical power calculation; no episode is omitted from reporting.
3. **Field target profile:** take the live source's selected profile at step 40, then calculate one-half the L1 distance between that fixed profile and each arm's normalized ten-band body at steps 40, 55 and 70. Smaller distance means the field profile is closer to that planted target. The post-pulse comparison is exploratory. The live-minus-shuffled and live-minus-uniform values are reported for all seeds and as descriptive means, with no p-values or independent-lineage claim.
4. **Persistence:** verify exact field checksum and PFM head across each serialized restore; report any source inputs admitted from the queue after new offers stop. Continuing response, if present, is not by itself evidence of an autonomous organisation because input is recorded in PFM by design.

The positive calibration is a deliberately constructed left-versus-right field contrast: from the same source state it produces different addresses; a valid offer must be seen in the actual Governor decision and PFM. A stale offer must be rejected before a runtime step. These engineering tests verify sensitivity and wiring; they do not create evidence of a discovered mind or target-directed tissue repair. An alignment or persistence effect also needs the matched controls; ordinary reaction–diffusion and the engineered memory mechanism remain alternative explanations. The field currently receives the Numogram through global zone-0/zone-9 coupling and sampled cells, so precise ten-band target control is not assumed. A failure of the body to match these profiles may reflect that interface.

No significance test or claim of a non-physical cause is registered. The engineered source demonstrates an address-responsive information pattern. A separate discovery study would need an independently specified source, positive and negative measurement controls, held-out patterns and challenges, and evidence of an organisation not simply inserted by the source or memory implementation. Retain failed runs and amendments; do not rerun or select favorable seeds without recording the change first.

Theory and analogy: Michael Levin, [*Ingressing Minds*](https://www.mdpi.com/2409-9287/11/5/161); Kriegman et al., [*Kinematic self-replication in reconfigurable organisms*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8670470/). The latter reports biological cell assemblies and physical self-replication; this is a synthetic computational analogue, not a replication of that biological experiment.
