# ANTENNA B: does the world stream's order carry structure? Registered 4 Nov 2026

## Why
Three Amelia-receiver designs could not hear a planted motif reliably:
- **THE ANTENNA** never checked.
- **ANTENNA II** failed its exact calibration.
- **ANTENNA III** passed only by a non-monotone fluke: 5% found, 2% and 10% not, and not the motif's own pairs.

Before asking Amelia to hear the world, B measures directly whether the world stream's sequence carries order structure at all, and where. If it does, we know what a receiver should hear. If it does not, Amelia is not to blame.

## The measurement (`structure.py`; no Amelia)
- **Elements.** Every zone n-gram, n = 2, 3, 4.
- **Segments.** The stream is cut into **M = 16 consecutive segments of L = 1,000 data**.
- **Comes through.** In each segment, an n-gram comes through when its count as received is greater than 0 and strictly greater than in each of **99 order-shuffles** of that segment. Under "order carries nothing" this is exact: p ≤ 1/100.
- **Recurs.** An n-gram recurs when it comes through in at least K segments. K is the smallest k with P(Binomial(16, 1/100) ≥ k) < 0.05/E, where E is the number of distinct n-grams observed.
- **Answer.** **STRUCTURED** (listing what recurs) if anything recurs, else **UNSTRUCTURED**.
- **Descriptive only.** Lag-1 to lag-5 mutual information of the whole stream, ranked against 999 shuffles.

## Calibration (`calibrate.py`; no world data)
- **Streams.** All are SHA-256-counter streams of 16,000 data, generated by `../antenna3/stream3.py`, as in ANTENNA III:
  - 10 clean streams;
  - motif 1-8-7-2 planted at ρ = 1%, 2%, 5% and 10%.
- **Pass.** Planted 2% → STRUCTURED with 1-8-7-2 among what recurs, and at most 1 of the 10 clean streams → STRUCTURED.
- **If it fails, B does not run on the world.**
- **Disclosure.** During development, clean stream c01 and planted 2% were run once as a smoke test (UNSTRUCTURED; STRUCTURED with 1-8-7-2 in 16/16). Nothing was changed after those runs.

## World test (registered here, sealed with an RFC 3161 token before its data exist)
- **Data.** The first **16,000** world-stream data (`world-live`, `experiments/world/collect.py`) first seen at or after the seal's timestamp.
- **Preparation.** They are taken in arrival order and reduced by AQ to digital roots exactly as THE ANTENNA did (`prepare_world.py`).
- **Primary answer.** `structure.py` with the parameters above, on the merged stream.
- **Descriptive, not decisive.** The same measurement on each source separately (markets, sun, words, earth, date), with M = the number of whole 1,000-datum segments available, if at least 5. This shows where any structure comes from.

## Reading it
- **STRUCTURED** means the order of the world's data carries recurring n-grams that no reordering of the same data produces. It does not by itself say why. Market prices moving in small steps, for example, give related digital roots.
- **The per-source view is the first step in reading it.** A recurring n-gram in a source with no such mechanism would be the interesting case.
- **UNSTRUCTURED** with this sensitivity means the stream's order carries no n-gram structure detectable at this scale.
