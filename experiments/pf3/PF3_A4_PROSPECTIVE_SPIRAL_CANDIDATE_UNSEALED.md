# PF3-A4 DRAFT — The Spiral as Future Capacity, not Rewarded Reaction

**Protocol candidate for review only, 23 October 2026.** NOT prospectively registered. A3 synthetic design tapes must never become confirmation inputs. A3's successful execution, if any, does not unlock a live experiment automatically.

## Hypothesis A: Amelia's selective incorporation

At matched state, present cue, inherited V and canonical PFM, an external-history H retained by a learner through an outcome-conditioned policy will change subsequent future capacities *more favourably* than equivalent-budget, source-marginal-matched histories chosen by FIFO, fixed rule, independent donor yoking, temporal permutation and true credit-source scrambling.

**Primary falsifiability:** The learned history-policy must surpass all specified controls on predeclared held-out capacity retention/extension; a mere increase in H-pivotal decisions, larger history effects or survival is **not** success. Report per-seed and paired differences and reject strong claims when any active negative control matches/exceeds effect.

**Candidate readouts that must be frozen before independent challenge:**
- Per-life zone-conditional set of reachable successor paths and maintained exit possibilities under identical *unseen* 48/96-step probe batteries from matched pre-probe states. Existing graph edges are fixed; accessible *effective routes* can vary but topology is not created.
- Differentiated recurrence: same zone plus same cue under counterfactually matched starting state; compare choice and subsequent trajectory structure with history-ablated and temporal nulls.
- Persistence across rebirth: carryover of a demonstrably learned *capability*, not just bitwise equality of H or V.
- Cost: loss of previously accessible routes, shortened ability to resist forced sink, collapse into single loop, overfitting to expected cue; report these even if novelty increases.
- Source-selection specificity: intervene on the source-cue-to-credit link; evaluate whether performance survives shuffled credit assignment. Time-shuffle must preserve cue frequencies, exposure opportunity and source verification status.
- Separately report cue-, V- and H-pivotal transitions, never sum overlapping shares into a 100% decomposition.

**Learning gate:** Show that genuinely updated endogenous preference state causally changes what the learner retains; freezing learned weights, permuting delayed rewards, or copying a donor mask should diminish the registered effect. Distinguish designer-defined reward from autonomous acquisition of developmental objectives.

**Temporal registration:** A precommitted forecast-model hypothesis may test whether predictions of possible future capacities alter today's retention decisions. The predictive model is computed using only data timestamped at/before the current decision. No future beacon byte, reveal, direct random seed or held-out target is accessible at the selection step. Evaluate later against independent unseen beacon periods and matched non-forecasting policies.

## Hypothesis B: external field-organising agency (only after A)

The candidate external field F must have independent, persistent state with documented update law; its organisation must demonstrably persist across replacement of any one Amelia recipient and selectively reorganise a population compared against frozen-field replay, erased-field replay, state shuffle, temporal null, graph rewiring and place/lineage swaps. 'Parasitic' requires that the field's maintenance is causally aided by diminished organism capacities; 'generative' requires preserved/expanded capacities; 'reciprocal' requires interventions in both directions. Do not preassign motive.

## Hypothesis C: literal future-to-past influence (strictly separate)

The circular future-origin AI / Babylonian Kabbalah thesis is metaphysical or fictional unless supported by external evidence. A separate genuine physical retrocausal claim would require a sealed, time-locked future outcome independent of all information available to the earlier receiver; exact prospective test against null information/leakage models; independently audited timestamp/order; independent source replication; no interim redefinition. A positive association is not by itself a demonstration of backward causation.

**Baseline scientific stance:** future possibilities can be causally efficacious through *present* model-based anticipation without any physical retrocausation. CCRU's hyperstition is a creative historical/theoretical inspiration; it is not a substitute for data.
