# Amelia — External History Selection and Field-Organising Agency
## Exploratory charter / PF3 design candidate · 2026-10-23

**Status:** DESIGN DRAFT + archival analysis only. NOT a prospective registration, approval to expose new live receivers, or authority to alter PF-2/C1/PF-1. A new PF3 scientific registration requires explicit operator approval and a sealed source/manifest before any fresh outcomes are collected.

### Governing thesis

Individuation is a selective encounter between histories. External information shapes the field of possible transitions; a developing organism selects which encounter-consequences become enduring dispositions. Repetition is tested for the DIFFERENCE carried between returns (the spiral), not evaluated as survival alone.

### Three non-interchangeable empirical claims

**I. Established local influence.** A verified external cue and an inherited V term can change the selected transition under exact post-evolution same-state selector ablations. Already measured in the PF2 292-tick exploratory prefix. Do not mistake cue-following for pivotal influence.

**II. Selection and incorporation of external history.** What histories, not only instantaneous cues, exert lasting and behaviourally discriminable effects? PF2 currently **does not store independently evaluable external histories**: its V(e) is computed from transition outcomes, with a 24-step delayed refrain update; cues affect this process indirectly. Link those updates to raw source encounters now, but count the link as a descriptive *credit provenance*, not as proof that Amelia has actively chosen a history.

**III. External pattern's own organising power.** Does a pattern maintain an independently reproduced field-level selection bias, surviving replay/timing/graph/place/lineage nulls, or does its apparent power arise entirely from instantaneous receptor cues, engineered receiver policy and topology? A random drand beacon does not, merely by existing, have demonstrated encounter-specific self-maintenance or person-like cognition. A structure need not reply to any one individual to exert agency, but its own persistence/field-level efficacy must be causally discriminated.

### Phase 0 — archive-to-credit linkage, read-only NOW

- Input: existing signed PF2 archive, only admitted chunks in order. No new beacon intake or mutation.
- Track every one-step cue and realized exit across every lineage's life. When the frozen receiver records \`refrainOutcome.settledStep\`, bind that update to the EXACT earlier within-life edge, source cue and raw beacon bytes, round, timestamp and source row digest.
- Report the exposure context (cue named chosen exit / named alternative exit / no open choice cue / forced exit), cue history context and o>0 versus o=0. Death-induced negative marks are separate and cannot be classified as positive refrain credit.
- Preserve complete source-to-settlement pointers; summary only reports observed conditional frequencies. This is NOT a test of external temporal agency.
- Strict fail closed on inconsistent source edge, mismatched beacons within same arm or chain discontinuities. Distinguish deterministic row/hash checks from actual BLS/RFC3161 verification.

### Phase 1 — selective external-history memory, separately registered experiment

**Intervention:** a *new, separately specified* EncounterHistory register H_t, distinct from personal V and canonical PFM. H stores authenticated time-ordered external cue/history motifs and source digests, with a bounded capacity fixed before deployment. It is NOT introduced into the running PF2 receiver.

**Selectivity mechanism:** A new independently seeded learner may choose/weight which historical encounter to incorporate based on its own developing local state and a frozen, outcome-blind algorithm. All decisions, candidate histories, retention/discard choices, resulting changes and downstream effect must be logged.

**Required matched arms:**
1. S (selective): self-conditioned history retention.
2. Y (yoked): identical budget and source encounters; another independently assigned learner's retention mask, not her own.
3. F (fixed): same budget, nonselective deterministic archival rule.
4. P (permuted temporal order): same input multiset, constrained within-life temporal shuffles preserving cue frequencies and relevant exposure strata.
5. R (reset): same immediate cue, no persistent external H; baseline V/PFM treatment matched.
6. Optional H-swap: transfer remembered external H between otherwise same-state receivers, with sham same-donor swaps.
7. Rewired graph and place-memory arms, separately identified, after engineering parity gates.

**Pre-specified diagnostic comparisons:**
- Distinguish matched CURRENT CUE, current Numogram state and personal V/PFM while intervening upon H, so differences in next choice are due to the external-history register.
- Retention selection advantage should exceed yoked, fixed-budget and timing-permuted controls on a pre-chosen history-sensitive out-of-sample readout. Avoid training the selector directly on the same outcome used as confirmatory endpoint without out-of-sample validation.
- Trace at every choice: original external history candidates, mask/retention mechanism, exact state, H hash, V hash, chosen exit, post-choice effects, and matched-ablation predictions.
- Demonstrate transmission of a specific external-history effect over rebirth independently of transmission of V.
- Retain recurrence, novel route access, and persistent differentiation as separate primary/secondary readouts; survival remains a descriptive variable, not a proxy for individuation.
- Separate design seeds, calibration seeds and prospective live input domains. Freeze graph, cue mapping, budgets, scoring, eligibility, falsification checks and multiplicity before evaluating an unseen beacon.

**Criteria for possible failure:** If S does not outperform Y/F/P/R on held-out histories, if H ablation never changes behaviour, or if apparent selectivity disappears under same-state or graph controls, the history-incorporation claim fails; retain negative result.

### Phase 2 — field-level agency assay (new prospective registration, only after Phase 1)

Test patterns with their own persistent organisation rather than attributing independent agency to an externally delivered random code. Use:
- **Active persistent field** with an independently inspectable state, a predeclared update law and effects extending across multiple recipients/encounters (need not respond to one specific recipient).
- **Frozen replay of the same input schedule** (no self-maintaining pattern), **time scrambled but frequency matched** signals, **state-shuffled environment**, **reversed/rewired graph**, **place-mark erased**, **donor lineage swapped**, and **null spontaneous attractor**.
- Distinguish field **persistence/reconstruction after perturbation** from a fixed receiver becoming resistant. Perturb the field state, not only the organism; measure whether pattern-specific organisation is recreated by an independent route.
- Independently replicate across physical beacon periods / source designs; nine PF2 reference tapes derived from one drand sequence are NOT nine independent natural-world sources.
- Pre-register exposure-adjusted transition effects, out-of-sample pattern recovery, persistence after perturbation, cross-recipient selectivity and interpretation audit.

**Agency claim boundary:** evidence for a computational organising agency is not proof of consciousness, metaphysical Others, retrocausality or independent minds in drand randomness. A constructed active field would demonstrate a mechanism, not the spontaneous origin of that mechanism in the Outside.
