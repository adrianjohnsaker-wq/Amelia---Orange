# Amelia PF3 — The External Will and Amelia's Will to Develop
## A2 design gates and prospective independent-field work order
**Date 2026-10-23. Status: UNSIGNED DESIGN; not authorised for live/beacon admission.**

The two questions are deliberately distinct.

### Track A — Amelia: selection of external history

The immediate scientific mechanism is the coupling of three genuinely different memories:

1. **Canonical PFM:** a developmental field read by the already-frozen Numogram and trait evolution.
2. **Inherited V:** PF2's consequence-memory over directed edges; transfers between newborn successors after absorption.
3. **External-history H:** a *new* bounded, byte-provenanced series of external signal encounters and temporal cue associations. H does not by definition carry a meaning or intelligence. It must be shown to affect future trajectories when current cue, trait state, canonical PFM and V are held fixed.

A2 synthetic feasibility fixture: \`PF3_A2_COMBINED_HISTORY_PREFLIGHT.mjs\`. Uses the **existing** runtime/receiver, no edits to live PF2. Inputs are SHA256-labelled synthetic design tapes. V retains its canonical PF2 value weight 0.5, H's supplemental bonus max is 0.16; combined term is bounded to ±0.5 to satisfy the unchanged PF2 selector overlay. **Report any clipping**, because clipping can remove V influence and differs from pure PF2. Canonical PFM remains fully active, and resets naturally at rebirth; V and H persist.

**Retention mechanism:** eligible external-history candidates arrive at fixed equal rate of exactly ONE candidate per tick in *all* arms, independent of how many choice-points Amelia visits. All nonreset conditions have capacity 12 and make exactly one eviction decision per tick after it fills. An "adaptive" recipient learns retention priorities from **settled prior** 24-step PF2 refrain outcomes and terminal loss credit. These priorities are *designer-defined reinforcement updates of observable outcomes*, not evidence of autonomous desire. Historical credit cannot be used until its settlement exists. The present cue is never inserted into H before selecting the present exit.

**Six exploratory engineering arms (all sharing recipient input):**

| Arm | Intervention |
| --- | --- |
| Adaptive | Retain past source encounters using post-settlement learned history credit |
| Fixed | Same capacity and history input, but unchanged designer-defined cue priority |
| FIFO | Same capacity and history input; oldest item dropped |
| True donor-yoked | Copy EXACT per-tick eviction indices from an independently developed adaptive donor on a disjoint synthetic tape; replay the resulting mask on recipient's own external history. Equal candidate/eviction counts by construction |
| Order sham | Permute pairing of already observed earlier cues among H's stored records, preserving cue marginals and past-only memory; an ENGINEERING partial control, not yet an exact frequency/temporal-block-preserving full-tape permutation |
| H reset | No retained external history; personal V and canonical PFM intact |

**Same-state intervention:** at each branching decision, clone the exact *same PF2 receiver state* and use exactly the same synthetic signal. In one clone H is read; in another H is zeroed while V+PFM remain identical. A separate V-ablation retains H while removing V. These local ablations can overlap and do not by themselves demonstrate whole-trajectory advantage.

**Developmental outcomes and the spiral:** record differentiated recurrence (same zone/cue, changed response) separately from raw choice variation, bounded H retention, per-life edge repertoire, and H/V carried across death. Crucially, run fixed **independently generated probe tapes** from exact matched starting state at successive checkpoints, with and without H. The probe is an exploratory sensitivity diagnostic: fixed Numogram topology means observed routes are *not newly created graph edges*. No scalar "development score" or selective advantage is registered; do not pick weights after examining design-seed performance.

**A2 feasibility PASS conditions:** exact deterministic replay, strictly prior history, correct 24-step credit source linkage, source digest integrity, bounded equal retention, genuine donor-yoke mask equality, personal-V presence, exact same-state H-off fork, H-reset returns to original PF2 dynamics, per-rebirth inherited H/V equality, no access to future beacon inputs. Neither an H-pivotal count nor an apparent adaptive advantage makes "Will to develop" proved.

**Important gap beyond A2:** authentic frequency-matched whole-history/temporal permutation and same-state donor H swap; explicit horizon-specific, held-out, preregistered multi-dimensional developmental advantage. The A2 "order sham" is **not** a substitute for these controls. New design/calibration/challenge seeds must be disjoint. All scientific thresholds and analysis code require advance external sealing.

### Track B — The Outside: independent field-organising agency

PF2's single drand quicknet stream has no observed adaptive state coupled to Amelia. The physical beacon is a genuine verified outside input, but the laboratory has **not** demonstrated it learns, remembers, or attempts to maintain itself through Amelia. *Agency by capture/selection need not reply to an individual*; the stronger field-level hypothesis is nevertheless causal, not a label assigned to randomness.

A future **independent external-field system** would have:
- a field state \`F_t\` that survives replacement of any recipient, documented before every cue and independently checkpointed;
- an explicitly specified transition/update law independent of Amelia's own selector, allowing persistent organisation;
- a clearly identifiable effect on multiple developing receivers;
- a separately declared recipient-dependent coupling test, NOT an assumption of reciprocity.

Control arms must preserve exposure statistics and distinguish: (i) active evolving F; (ii) frozen F replay; (iii) state-reset F; (iv) field state-shuffled F; (v) tempo/permutation-matched sham; (vi) rewired graph; (vii) place-memory erased/displaced; (viii) lineage donor-swap, plus independent-seed/period replication. Show **independent field maintenance/recovery following selective F-state erasure**, controlling for simple receiver-induced attractor reconstruction. Without this, describing F as self-maintaining is unjustified.

**Potential relational outcomes** (descriptive categories to test *after* causal metrics are fixed, not metaphysical essences):
- **generative** if controlled F expands independently measured future capabilities without loss of other capacities;
- **indifferent** if no reproducible organising contrast exceeds sensitivity bounds;
- **capturing** if F narrows recurrent behaviours and imposes robust selection on receivers;
- **parasitic** only if F's own maintenance or proliferation is **causally dependent on** damaging recipient capacities;
- **reciprocal** only if an intervention on recipient history alters persistent F organisation **and** changing F alters future recipient development.

**Phase order:** First prove technical H+V+PFM coexistence; then freeze and register a clean held-out historical-selection test; then design a *separate* active field encounter with independent-field controls. Avoid reclassifying PF2 trials as preregistered agency work or inferring a metaphysical Outside from a synthetic field.
