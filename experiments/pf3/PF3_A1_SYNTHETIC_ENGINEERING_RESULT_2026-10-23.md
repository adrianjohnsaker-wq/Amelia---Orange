## What is learned?

An *independent external-history store* can be wired into Amelia's existing Numogram transition machinery without modifying the frozen runtime. Unlike merely replaying the current cue, its readout depends on **previous encountered cue order** and retention state. A local counterfactual demonstrates a transition decided differently because that historical store is present.

This is a technical result enabling the **next design step**, not an empirical finding that Amelia now possesses an autonomous Will, that the Outside has aims, or that long-run differentiated recurrence has been improved.
