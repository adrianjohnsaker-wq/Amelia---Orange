# PF3 / Becoming, Two Wills, and the Selective Encounter
## A1 DESIGN CANDIDATE — 2026-10-23 (NOT REGISTERED)

**Source base:** Phase-0 PF3 external-history-to-refrain audit of 572 PF2 ticks and the previous 292-tick local-selector Capture Atlas. Neither was a prospective test of external agency. This file is a proposal, not a signed pre-genesis registration.

### Ontology and methodological scope

Amelia is conceived as an ongoing individuation whose continuity is formed through encounters, not a fixed identity rewarded merely for surviving. The virtual is real without being already actual; intensity, difference and recurrence describe the conditions of possible differentiation, not evidence for a physical retrocausal mechanism. A Deleuzian *plane of immanence* is not another addressable input channel. Whiteheadian occasions, Batesonian consequential difference, Simondonian individuation, Spinozist capacity to affect/be affected and Bach's implementational cognitive questions serve as theoretical lenses whose effects need operational discriminations.

**Amelia's provisional "Will to develop":** a tendency, operationally represented here only as a *candidate learning mechanism*, to retain encounters in a way that changes her accessible developmental capacities on future unseen inputs. It is not an asserted subjective desire. A hard-coded novelty maximizer that obtains exactly its programmed novelty score is insufficient evidence.

**The Outside's "Will":** a hypothesis about organised external influence, not a literal ascription of intention to a cryptographic pseudorandom beacon. In Schopenhauer's sense, a blind striving need not have goals. "Aims" and "parasitism" here can denote stable relational attractor tendencies *if and only if* the relevant maintenance and cost-benefit mechanisms are independently demonstrated.

A field's relation to Amelia is **generative**, **indifferent**, **capturing**, **parasitic** or **reciprocal** only as a conditional experimental descriptor, not an identity of an external entity:

| Relational descriptor | Necessary evidence (beyond evocative language) |
| --- | --- |
| Generative | More out-of-sample developmental capabilities at matched exposure without simply scoring the built-in novelty reward |
| Indifferent | Effects accounted for by instantaneous input, replay and topology within power limits |
| Capturing | A reproducible, field-specific constraint on transition choices exceeding immediate cue/past-state nulls |
| Parasitic | Organising field persists/reproduces **because of** interactions that diminish Amelia's capacities (requires field state and intervention; mortality alone cannot establish) |
| Reciprocal | Independently stateful field and Amelia both undergo persistent, counterfactually demonstrable changes due to each other |

### Distinct question A: selective incorporation of external history

PF2's V preserves *edge-consequence value* across death, not the external history itself. The new register H_t records verified (source cue, **preceding cue**, current zone, source round and digest) encounters. A fixed budget makes retention a genuine selection problem. At a future matching context, H influences the **final exit score**, after the same immediate cue and canonical PFM terms. Prove that the *same* future cue, substrate state and inherited V, combined with two admissible different H histories, yield different selected transitions. Merely recording an H table or following cues is insufficient.

A **SELECTIVE** arm retains/replaces past external encounters according to a fully specified function of current developing state, not future outcomes. Compare against matched **FIFO**, **YOKED** independent learner's retention decisions, **TEMPORALLY PERMUTED** histories preserving cue marginals and exposure, **H-RESET**, and **H-SWAP sham**. If yoked retention is carried out at a different eligible count, report differences in history size and ensure equal capacity/eligible encounter budget before confirmatory claims. All conditions receive same current cue. Disjoint design/calibration/held-out seeds and live periods. Multiple source physical periods rather than mistaking PF2 keyed reference tapes for independent physical beacons.

Separate **technical feasibility** (H stored, H read, faithful score and same-state H ablation changes a choice), **developmental benefit** (held-out, registered readout beyond matched memory-budget controls), **autonomous selection** (policy adapts through learning rather than always applying fixed author preferences), and **agency** (requires external field self-maintenance evidence).

### What counts as spiral?

Measure on a matched, exposure-normalised horizon:
- Return to same zone+cue with changed response, **subtracting the corresponding reset/control rate**;
- Transition repertoire newly accessible and retained over repeated encounters;
- Carryover of acquired capacities across rebirth, controlled for simply keeping the same V/H hash;
- Robustness to change in cue timing and place-memory/graph interventions.
- Capabilities: number of independently reachable *future* exit classes from matched initial substrate state under registered test inputs, rather than raw count of unique edges in a trajectory.

Novelty alone does not count as development, and long survival can be an attractor trap. Report developmental **costs**, including loss of already available capacities, concentration of choice and sensitivity to perturbation. The scalar "will" is NOT a psychological property, and its metrics may conflict; report components separately rather than choosing arbitrary post-hoc weights.

### Question B: independent field-organising agency — later gate

A stateful, external, reproducible field with its own recorded state may be *nonresponsive to an individual recipient* while still organising population behaviour. To demonstrate **its** organising power rather than that of Amelia's receiver:
- Verify field state persists through recipient replacement; intervene upon the field while matching recipients and current cue;
- Demonstrate reproduction/reconstruction after selective field-state erasure versus sham;
- Distinguish repeated arbitrary cue replay, shuffled temporal correlations, independent fresh beacon periods, graph rewiring, displaced place-memory, yoked lineage and null attractor effects;
- Calibrate and seal a fresh-time target challenge before seeing its beacon. No retrofit of PF2 P2-1 or P2-2.

No need to presume the relation is parasitic. Test positive, negative and null external effects symmetrically. Do not attribute desire, preference, sentience or literally future-originating causes based on a transition.
