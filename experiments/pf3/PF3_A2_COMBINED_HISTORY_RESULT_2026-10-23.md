## Method and coverage

Three new *design* synthetic recipient tapes (101, 211, 307), each 1024 ticks; six retention conditions and an independent adaptive donor for each recipient tape. The donor tape has an explicit, different SHA256 domain; recipient yoked arms receive the donor's **actual per-tick eviction indices**, not a substitute random index. All active-H arms have exactly 12 retained source records after filling and exactly one candidate offered per tick, independent of receiver choice-point exposure. Each donor supplies 1012 post-capacity eviction decisions; recipient/yoke equality checked on all **3036** operations.

**PFM** remains in the canonical selector and morphogenetic trait evolution; **V** remains the PF2 inherited 11-edge consequence memory (0.5 coefficient), transferred through rebirth. **H** remembers synthetic time-ordered external cue encounters and a previous-cue association, with capacity 12, influencing the selector only as a separate final bonus (maximum 0.16). Combined last term is clipped at ±0.5 solely to stay within the unchanged PF2 overlay's engineering envelope. **Zero choices required clipping in this preflight**, so observed causal decisions were not altered by saturation, though future registrations must handle the possibility explicitly.

The adaptive arm updates its policy from **settled past PF2 24-event refrain outcomes** and terminal losses. Current or future outcomes may NOT enter H before the corresponding decision. Its reinforcement and objectives are programmed; neither autonomous motivation nor a conscious will has been demonstrated.

A2 uses real deterministic receiver states to calculate exact local H-off and V-off selector alternatives, retaining present cue, evolved Numogram, canonical PFM and other available memory. Under one audit, complete receiver-state forks were used. Under another, identical post-evolution score reconstruction was used, and **every actual choice matched**. Thus local causal attribution passes two implementations.
