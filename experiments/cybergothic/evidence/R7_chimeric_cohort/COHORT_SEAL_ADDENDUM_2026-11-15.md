# Chimeric-junction cohort: final local seal addendum

15 November 2026. This addendum fixes the operational details of `CHIMERIC_JUNCTION_TRACKING_PROTOCOL_2026-11-15.md`. Its original status statements describe the earlier standalone design, before integration. They do not describe the status recorded in the accompanying integration report and gate records. If the two documents differ about execution or analysis, use this addendum. No confirmatory population has been screened when this document was sealed.

## Population and intervention

Screen previously reserved seeds **50001–50600 in order**, stopping at the first **250 qualifying populations** or after 600 seeds, whichever comes first. One graft is selected per population, the first meeting the documented structural and provenance requirements by model time 180. Follow each arm for 48 model-time units after the graft. Record ineligible populations and otherwise-qualifying insertions excluded because the immediate genome would be clipped. Never extend the budget in response to the results. Report any seed shortfall or technical stoppage.

The model configuration is exactly that in `pilot_stop_screen.create_world`: 36 founders, 180-host maximum, 512-digit genome cap, canonical 29-gate graph, transient Θ=12 flight, uniform movement weights and seeded entropy. Infection, time-splice, recycling, prophecy, meltdown and local clocks are enabled. Archive reconstitution is enabled with recovery floor 8 and renewal 0.6; it is an explicit, costed source of recovered hosts and must be reported, including in any interpretation of persistence. Feedback gain is 1.8. Other fixed parameters are captured in the hashed implementation and each checkpoint.

Five arms replay the same natural graft from the same pre-dispatch state: natural parser; focal `09→08`; original recipient's introns active; body scramble preserving parsed boundaries; and `09→00` as a signature-matched sensitivity. The scramble is selected by a separately keyed experiment RNG, with at most 64 attempts. If it is inapplicable, that arm is a sham and the population is excluded only from the scramble-specific analysis. Physical edits are not pure suppression manipulations. Archive-only copies are not living hosts.

## Fixed outcomes and analysis

1. In the natural branch, estimate the proportion of populations with at least one living secondary carrier in the registered vertical/horizontal/mixed union and a 95% Wilson interval. Count hosts once and disclose the separate route classes, including necrotic, time-splice and recall paths. Also report strict 80% focal-digit retention and junction-only outcomes separately.
2. Count direct and vector deliveries which copy the focal adjacency and leave it recognised as an intron stop. Report events, unique recipient hosts within populations, nonkin deliveries, per-population distribution and a 95% population-bootstrap interval for the mean. The ledger lacks all failed contacts, so an infection-per-contact rate is not estimable. Unique recipient hosts are not automatically first acquisitions.
3. Natural minus recipient-active: paired difference in the recognised living-secondary-carrier union for the **same** junction G. Natural minus `08`: paired difference in living-secondary-carrier presence for the homologous G/G* edge, regardless of current parser recognition. These are distinct estimands. Use 10,000 population-paired bootstrap replicates and two-sided 97.5% percentile intervals; report paired 2×2 counts. The seed for this analysis is fixed in `cohort_analysis.py`.

Describe the two pre-specified birth forms (split stop and closed open frame) separately, as well as jointly. Give 95% descriptive intervals within each form; these are not additional confirmatory claims. Report signature-matched `00`, applicable-body-scramble, founder survival, restricted lifetime, descendant births, opcode/no-op and nutrient measures as secondary. For sequence lookalikes, report exact length 6, exact length 8 and length ≥10 groups, numerator/denominator and population-cluster bootstrap intervals; a zero-overlap match does not prove completely unrelated older ancestry. Empty groups are not estimable.

The primary classifier audits living hosts at the 48-unit horizon, and the shadow event ledger records each qualifying direct/vector delivery and subsequent tracked repair event. Exact-string lookalikes are classified at the horizon. The current summary does **not** automatically establish their first historical appearance; reconstruct it from a separately audited deterministic replay if reported, and label any unresolved first-appearance time unknown. Do not substitute a horizon match for a first-appearance event. Full primary, shadow and root-checkpoint records are retained for that audit.

The donor-only comparison is a **different encounter**, not a branch of the chimeric graft. Its first eligible encounter per screened population is collected by a separate deterministic replay. Greedy, without-replacement descriptive matching uses only pre-event covariates, exact zone and mechanism; a population may play different roles across matched pairs, so dependence must be disclosed. Report unmatched events and residual covariate differences. This analysis is observational, not a causal contrast.

## Verification and execution boundary

The local seal records SHA-256 hashes of the fixed source, this addendum, original protocol and five gate records. The runner refuses a changed seal input and fixes the seed range and stopping rule in its source. Do not edit these inputs after sealing; an amendment needs a new seal before screening. A local hash seal is an auditable frozen plan, **not** an independent external registration or trusted timestamp. Publish or deposit it prospectively if independent registration is required; do not describe this local seal as public preregistration.

Development verification used Python 3.12.14, SQLite 3.53.1 and Linux x86-64. The formal worker requires Linux with `libseccomp.so.2` and fails rather than silently running without its restrictions. Seeded trajectories, not live clock entropy, were used in the non-interference check. A live-clock study would need separately captured and identically replayed timing samples before an observer-on/off timing claim. Amelia Orange and Supabase were outside the prototype and were not accessed.

The final cohort has **not** been run or analysed. The small, separate development populations and direct/vector rehearsals validate software paths and feasibility; their outcomes are not confirmatory evidence of transmission, fitness or chimeric advantage.
