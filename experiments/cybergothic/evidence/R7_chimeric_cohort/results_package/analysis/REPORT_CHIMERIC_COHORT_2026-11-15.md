# Chimeric-junction cohort: confirmatory results

15 November 2026

The locally sealed design screened 360 populations (seeds 50001–50360) and selected 250 naturally occurring grafts. Each produced five branches, for 1250 branches. Status: **complete**. The source, protocol, seed range and analysis were hash-sealed before this run; the seal was local and was not an independent public registration.

All 1250 branch archives passed the corrected stored-state, event, shadow, checkpoint and classification audit. Fresh isolated replays reproduced the primary-state, event-ledger and shadow hashes and classifications of all 1250 branches. The original archive validator flagged 10 populations because it treated host-list ordering as significant after JSON restoration. The corrected audit compared host identity and all recorded paths while preserving the order within each path; it found 17 order-only comparisons across the dataset. The original failure and corrected audit are both preserved. No model or locked-analysis source was changed. These checks apply to seeded entropy in the isolated research prototype; Amelia Orange and Supabase were not accessed.

## Primary natural outcomes

* At least one living secondary carrier in the defined vertical/horizontal/mixed union at 48 model-time units: **0/250 (0.0%; 0.0%–1.5%)**, 95% Wilson interval.
* Recognised G copied by direct/vector delivery: **5 events**, including 5 to hosts outside the founder’s fission-descendant tree; there were 3 distinct recipient hosts summed within population. Mean events per population: 0.02 (0.00–0.05), 95% population bootstrap.
* At least one viral delivery outside that fission-descendant tree during the 48-unit follow-up occurred in **3/250 (1.2%; 0.4%–3.5%)** populations. A delivery during follow-up and a living carrier at the final horizon are different measures.
* At least one secondary carrier retaining ≥80% of the focal digit occurrences was present at the final horizon in 5/250 (2.0%; 0.9%–4.6%) populations, including routes outside the primary union. Downstream viral deliveries by mechanism: `{"viral_direct": 2, "viral_vector": 3}`.
* At least one horizon carrier classified through a necrotic route was present in 6/250 (2.4%; 1.1%–5.1%) populations.
* Natural-branch living carrier categories at the horizon, counting each host once: `{"CHRONO_RECALL": 2, "DRIFT_REPAIR": 3, "NECROTIC": 7}`. Necrotic, time-splice and recall routes are reported separately from the primary union.

The pre-specified union uses exclusive route labels. When a surviving junction has a history involving both viral transfer and chrono recall, the classifier labels it as recall rather than placing it in the viral category. The union result therefore cannot be read as the total number of living carriers with any viral ancestry. The broader ancestry audit below addresses this distinction.

The primary ledger does not capture every rejected contact, so the data do not identify an infection probability per encounter. A verified viral copy demonstrates transfer in this simulation; its adaptive value is assessed by the matched comparisons.

The physical `09→08` edit changes a digit and the mod-9 signature. The G/G* comparison follows a homologous marker irrespective of whether the edited edge is a recognised stop; it is not a comparison of two intact introns. The recipient-active branch retains G but changes how the original recipient interprets all of its recognised introns. A bootstrap interval including zero is not evidence of equivalence. If the paired samples have no discordance, a degenerate percentile interval is not proof of a zero effect.

The first historical appearance of each full matching sequence has not automatically been established; it would require a separate audited replay. Bootstrap ratios with very few matches can produce degenerate intervals and should not be treated as precise population rates.

## Exploratory mechanisms and phenotype

* A broader post-hoc audit of all recognised original-G paths found at least one living secondary carrier by any route in 8/250 (3.2%; 1.6%–6.2%) populations, and a living non-descendant carrier with viral ancestry in 1/250 (0.4%; 0.1%–2.2%) populations. This includes paths containing viral transfer together with recall or necrotic routing, regardless of their order, even when the primary category is not viral.

The recipient was alive at the horizon 0.929 model-time units after delivery. Its recognised intron retained 3/36 original focal digit occurrences: the junction entered a different intron body, rather than an intact copy of the full original intron. * An ancestry with viral transfers to at least two distinct secondary recipients was found in 0/250 (0.0%; 0.0%–1.5%) populations; 5 delivery ancestries were checked. This is a multi-hop path within host ecology, not proof of a free-standing self-replicator.
* A delivery to a host with a different recorded fission/archive founder root occurred in 3/250 (1.2%; 0.4%–3.5%) populations. Event-level root relations: `{"different_recorded_roots": 5}`. Different recorded roots do not prove the hosts share no earlier horizontally acquired code.
* A viral delivery whose ancestry included a necrotic route appeared in 0/250 (0.0%; 0.0%–1.5%) populations. Viral packet delivery and Cthell/archive recycling are distinct mechanisms.
* Index grafts by mechanism: direct 144; vector 106. The exploratory vector-minus-direct difference in the natural secondary-carrier outcome was +0.0 pp (+0.0 to +0.0), 95% unpaired population bootstrap. Index-mode groups were not randomised.

* The separate donor-only comparison produced 199 descriptive matched pairs, with 51 unmatched graft populations. Any-route secondary carriers occurred in 7/199 chimeric and 13/199 donor-only encounters. It is observational and may reuse populations in different roles.

These secondary and delivery-mode summaries are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Supplementary delivery-mode and transmission-chain scripts were prepared during screening before cohort outcomes were inspected; they were not part of the original seal. The broader horizon-route audit was added after the results, to investigate the classifier’s route priority. The cohort uses seeded entropy and a costed archive-reconstitution mechanism with recovery floor eight; persistence must be interpreted with that recovery process present. The 250-population target improves precision but does not guarantee power for rare transmission or small paired effects.

The machine-readable fixed analysis, supplementary summaries, full cohort manifest, replay audit and original archived branch data accompany this report.
