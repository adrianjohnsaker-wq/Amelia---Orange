# Chimeric-junction observer: integration and pre-run gates

15 November 2026

The provenance observer is integrated into a separate copy of the self-parsing Cybergothic Numogram prototype. All five pre-run gates passed. The 250-population confirmatory cohort is fixed in a local hash seal; it has **not** been run. A local seal is not an independent public registration.

## What now follows a junction

The digit genome and parser remain the executable substrate. A separate shadow tape records the origin and exact copied occurrence of each digit, and gives each adjacent pair its own lineage and copy-occurrence identity. This lets the audit distinguish a particular newly grafted `09` from an identical-looking stop that appeared independently, or from the same older digits subsequently rejoined into a new boundary. The observer also records whether a living carrier acquired its junction through fission, viral delivery, time-splice, necrotic recycling or recall; those routes are kept distinct.

The shadow tape follows direct and vector infection, fission, two-way splicing, point and necrotic mutation, circular payloads, genome-cap truncation, corpses and archive storage, reconstitution, and saved histories used by replay or chrono recall. A qualifying G is selected only if its new stop is a physically verified mixed recipient/viral boundary in a recognised intron of at least six digits. Length alone is never treated as proof of descent.

The model never consults provenance while deciding instructions, movement, energy or random draws. The research intervention selects its target using the recorded graft and can change the genome or interpretation in a specified branch; the provenance itself is observational.

The non-interference result is for **seeded** entropy. It does not establish that a live timing clock would produce the same values when the observer adds overhead. Such a test needs a captured entropy stream replayed identically in both runs. Matching hashes also verify these runs and the tested execution paths, not the absence of every conceivable bug.

The integration, comparator and analysis suites passed 27 directed tests. A fresh-process restart of the included example checkpoint reproduced all three expected hashes and the classification at the exact recorded horizon.

## Development feasibility and end-to-end rehearsal

On separate development seeds 45101–45140, 25 of 40 populations produced an eligible natural graft by time 180. Seventeen closed an open frame and eight split an existing stop; 16 arrived by direct infection and nine by vector delivery. This is a feasibility screen, not an estimate from the future confirmatory sample and not an efficacy result.

Two additional development rehearsals used one direct and one vector graft. Each ran all five branches from the same scheduler-safe pre-dispatch checkpoint. The natural branch matched its original, uninterrupted primary trajectory. An archive audit verified all ten saved branches, including their compressed primary event/state and observer data, hashes, restored classifications and root checkpoints. The pre-event donor-only comparison was also exercised. The development results were not used to select a favourable cohort outcome.

## Sealed next cohort

The fixed plan screens seeds 50001–50600 in order, taking the first 250 qualifying populations, or reporting the shortfall after 600 seeds. Each qualifying graft is followed for 48 model-time units under the natural, `09→08`, recipient-active, body-scramble and `09→00` arms. The two primary paired comparisons concern the recognised original G under natural versus recipient-active interpretation, and homologous G/G* edge propagation under natural versus physical rupture. They use 97.5% paired-bootstrap intervals. Natural transmission prevalence, viral delivery counts, route distinctions and sequence lookalikes are recorded separately.

The included addendum fixes analysis details, exclusions, comparison groups and limits. In particular, the current automatic sequence-lookalike audit is at the final horizon; a claimed historical first appearance needs an additional audited replay. Archive reconstitution remains enabled as a costed, explicitly reported part of this experimental ecology. These tests establish an instrument and a prospective design; they do **not** yet establish that chimeric junctions spread, improve fitness or act as adaptive traits.

`FINAL_COHORT_SEAL.json` records hashes of the fixed source, plan, gate records and supporting rehearsal evidence. The runner checks these before the formal cohort and does not allow its seed range or target to be overridden. Independent public preregistration would be a separate step.
