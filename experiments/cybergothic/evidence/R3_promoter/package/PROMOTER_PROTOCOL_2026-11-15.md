# Conditional viral promoters: locally fixed outcome plan

**15 November 2026 · isolated cybergothic research prototype**

The prior test found an immediate benefit of experienced fragments and an engineered increase in the availability of repeated copies, but did not establish a sustained fitness advantage over a shuffled-code echo. Environmental drift, pointer starvation and generic benefits of injection are hypotheses, not established explanations. This study tests whether conditioning virtual viral instructions on Numogram location improves recipient behavior under matched code and costs.

## Engine and promoter

The prior developed research ecology is reused unchanged: the 29-gate graph, asynchronously scheduled hosts, mutable matrix, contagion, necrotic reactor and externally replenished virtual nutrients. The prototype remains separate from Amelia Orange, her Governor, gates, database and scheduled tasks.

The experimental cassette consists of **one header digit plus four payload digits**. At first delivery, the header is the recipient's current zone (0–9); the payload is the previously selected experienced code, or its digit-preserving shuffle. The header is regulatory rather than an opcode. Existing ancestry slots are repurposed as cassette-position labels in the experimental branch; they identify the cassette and propagate through the substrate's inheritance and transfer rules. Thus this implementation reads the encoded header digit, but its interpreter also relies on **non-digit structural metadata**; it is not yet a fully self-parsing, digits-only promoter grammar. Original donor ancestry remains in the recorded candidate data.

Before every agent action, the interpreter scans from the instruction pointer to the next executable instruction. For a complete contiguous five-label cassette, it reads the actual current header digit. If a cassette's labels are split or its header is missing, its tagged payload is fail-closed until a complete cassette is restored. Header or payload digits may mutate. New agents and recipients carrying the labelled cassette use the same rules. Ordinary unlabelled digits are still executable, even if their values happen to equal viral digits. A scan costs no additional virtual time; if all instructions are blocked, the host pays basal metabolism, ages and is rescheduled without an opcode or nutrient harvest. All arms use this same machinery.

## Conditions

Each of 60 fresh seeds **35001–35060** starts from the corrected strict first-contact sampler: 36 founders, 30-unit warm-up, alternating-ID shock and 512-digit cap applied after warm-up. Sample at most two different recipients before the environmental reversal (time 30–90) and two after (126–198), requiring documented donor experience and space for the five-digit cassette. Same starting state, host, header, insertion cost and packet apply across five matched branches:

1. **Open:** the experienced payload is permitted in every zone.
2. **Zone promoter:** the experienced payload is permitted only in the zone specified by the current header digit.
3. **Rotated promoter:** the same payload and header are permitted only in `(header + 1) mod 10`, a deliberately mismatched context control.
4. **Zone/shuffled:** the zone promoter controls the digit-preserving shuffled payload.
5. **Syzygy promoter:** the experienced payload is permitted in the header zone and its nine-sum twin.

Every branch uses the same active maintenance rule: from the recipient's fourth action, and every third recipient action thereafter, insert a new five-digit cassette at its current pointer for **0.05 virtual energy**. The echoed header is the initial recipient zone; the payload follows that branch's initial choice. Attempts are skipped if unaffordable or cap-limited. All controls include the same header, maintenance rule, initial transfer and interpreter overhead; later doses may diverge after their ecologies diverge. No claim of a free or naturally evolved promoter is made.

The continuing population used to locate later first contacts receives the original balanced randomized experienced/pointer/shuffled assignment without the experimental promoter, as in the previous studies. Initial candidates with identical experienced and shuffled digits remain in the analysis. Environmental signals are checked for equality across each encounter's futures.

## Outcomes

The primary outcome is total virtual nutrient uptake by the **original recipient over 24 model-time units**, with no further intake after death. Three planned paired contrasts are **zone minus open**, **zone minus rotated**, and **zone minus zone/shuffled**. Average within each independently seeded population, then average the population means. Use 10,000 seed-clustered bootstrap resamples and two-sided **98.33%** intervals (family adjustment for three comparisons). Seeds with no eligible contacts are reported and omitted from recipient-conditional means; intervals are approximate.

Report recipient survival, action counts, payload execution, suppressed payload opportunities, fragment fail-closure, energy costs and delivered or skipped echoes. Syzygy versus exact-zone and all 12- and 36-unit outcomes are exploratory, as are subgroup results. Count execution and suppression rule violations across *all* agents carrying labelled code, not just the original recipient. Initial encounter zone is a design choice, not evidence that donor experience was adapted to that zone; this experiment does not by itself establish environmental drift.

Fixture seed 34991 and pilot seeds 34992–34993 were inspected before this plan; pilot output came from an intermediate version before a code organization refactor, whose exact intermediate source was not archived separately.
