# Viral code echo: local pre-outcome plan

**15 November 2026 · isolated developed cybergothic research prototype**

The previous encounter study found an immediate recipient response to experienced code and no clearly separated 12-unit survival or total-uptake effect. That result alone does not establish rapid washout. This study measures the original fragment's retention and tests an explicitly engineered, energy-costed code echo over a longer horizon.

No Amelia Orange state, Governor, gate, database or scheduled task is involved. The research engine retains its existing costed archive reactor and external nourishment inputs. This is not a test of an unassisted perpetual ecology.

## Encounter and branches

Use 60 previously uninspected independent seeds 34001–34060. Per seed, use the prior strict first-contact world: 36 founders, a 30-unit warm-up, a shared alternating-ID shock, and the same post-warm-up 512-digit cap. Sample up to two different recipients in time 30–90 and two different recipients in time 126–198. Sample only a first-contact packet with documented donor experience and three compatible candidate fragments. The two phases may contribute different counts. The continuation used to locate subsequent encounters receives the earlier balanced randomized experienced/pointer/shuffled assignment.

Immediately before an eligible infection, copy the complete world and insert the **same experienced fragment** at the selected host pointer in all three copies. Equal initial packet energy transfer applies. Follow the original host ID and surrounding ecology for 36 model-time units:

1. **Natural:** allow subsequent ecology without experimental echoes.
2. **Experienced echo:** starting at the recipient's fourth action, and again every three recipient actions thereafter, insert the experienced fragment at its current pointer immediately before that action. Debit 0.05 virtual energy per insertion.
3. **Shuffled echo:** use the same schedule, cost and insertion process but insert the prior study's digit-and-ancestry-preserving shuffled fragment. Both echo arms received the same *experienced* first insertion. The shuffled comparator isolates the effect of echo content/order under this particular refresh strategy; the echo mechanism itself is a designed intervention.

An echo is skipped if insufficient energy or insertion would exceed the genome cap. Count delivered and skipped echoes, actual total costs and host actions; differing later recipient fates can produce unequal realized doses. A shuffled fragment identical to the experienced sequence remains eligible and is reported. Natural and echo branches can diverge after their common starting RNG state. Exogenous modeled environment signals are checked equal among each encounter's branches.

## Tracking

At the initial insertion, assign distinct observer-only negative labels to its digit ancestry slots in each experimental copy. Echo copies receive new, distinct labels, and preserve the original relative labeling under the shuffle. Existing source ancestry is retained in the encounter record; these special labels replace ancestry labels only inside the experimental copies. Tracking counts an intact copy when correctly ordered labels and digits are contiguous in the original recipient's genome. It distinguishes the original transmitted label lineage from copies introduced by the echo; it records first loss of the original lineage while the host remains alive, later reappearance, actions executing correctly tagged digits, and the event accompanying a detected change. Death and no observed loss by the horizon are separate outcomes. The labels propagate through the existing simulated inheritance pathways, but a textually identical unlabelled code fragment does not count as that lineage.

The instrumentation is checked on an actual encounter in all three arms by comparing the complete event history and final state with and without labels after projecting out ancestry fields. It does not claim to validate every possible input; the full source and traces are included. The old module is not altered.

## Primary outcomes and analysis

At **24** model-time units, score dead recipients zero for code retention and no further nutrient uptake after death. There are two planned paired comparisons:

1. The fraction of recipients retaining at least one intact ordered copy from the original or an echo: **experienced echo minus natural**. This quantifies engineered maintenance; it is not evidence that the originally transmitted physical copy survived.
2. Total recipient nutrient uptake: **experienced echo minus shuffled echo**. A positive result supports a functional advantage of this particular ordered echo over its digit-preserving control in this environment.

Average encounter-level paired differences within each independent seed, then average the seed means. Exclude and report seeds that produced no eligible encounters. Use 10,000 seed-cluster bootstrap resamples with two-sided **97.5%** percentile intervals for the two primary comparisons (Bonferroni). Intervals and all model results are approximate and conditional on the implemented ecology.

Secondary exploratory measures at 3, 6, 12, 24 and 36 units include survival, original-label retention, any-copy retention, tagged-digit executions, total uptake, dose and cost; first-loss event categories compete with host death. Secondary contrasts use unadjusted 95% clustered intervals and are not confirmatory. Comparisons restricted to surviving hosts or later observed actions are descriptive, since survival and number of actions can themselves differ across arms.
