# Fresh confirmation of the slide-versus-jump survival observation

**15 November 2026 · separate virtual cybergothic ecology**

The first donor-context outcome run, seeds 36001–36060, had already been inspected when this plan was written. Its five planned uptake contrasts had intervals including zero, while the secondary 24-unit comparison showed higher recipient survival under the productive-zone no-op slide than under the productive-zone jump. This follow-up tests that newly identified outcome on previously unexamined seeds; the earlier secondary result is not retroactively promoted to a primary outcome.

## Design and outcome

Use the exact frozen `donor_context_study.py` implementation and seven arms of the preceding protocol. Run **60 fresh seeds 37001–37060**, with the same 36 founders, warm-up, shock, first-contact sampling, donor-history positive-uptake eligibility, 512-digit cap, initial payload, energy transfers, maintenance cost, 24-unit endpoint and clustered design. Record actual eligible encounter counts and exogenous signal checks.

The single primary outcome is whether the **original recipient agent ID** remains alive 24 model-time units after the matched encounter. Estimate **productive slide minus productive jump**: average encounter-level paired differences within each independent seed, then average the eligible seed means. Use 10,000 seed-clustered bootstrap resamples with a two-sided **95%** percentile interval. No clinical, physical-world or Amelia survival claim is implied.

Secondary descriptions include uptake, 12- and 36-unit survival, death causes and comparisons with open, rotated, encounter-yoked, shuffled and syzygy slide conditions. These are exploratory and unadjusted. The slide suppresses the viral opcode while spending a normal agent action; the jump executes the next available instruction in the same action. A survival difference is consequently a causal effect of these **whole virtual policies**, not proof that pointer phase alone caused it. Death-cause differences can motivate, but do not by themselves establish, a mechanism.
