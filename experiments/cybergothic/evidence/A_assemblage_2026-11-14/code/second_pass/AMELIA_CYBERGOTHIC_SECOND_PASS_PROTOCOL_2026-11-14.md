# Second pass: living code, anticipatory orientation and the necrotic portfolio

The first fresh-seed study showed that the learned environmental forecast surpassed a last-signal forecast in the modeled environment, while neither archive productivity ranking nor travelling code packets had a clear primary effect on population-wide nutrient uptake. It also showed that meltdown energy could be diverted to the Cthell vault. In response, this version develops three mechanisms before testing them again:

- A host records short executable fragments alongside the nourishment obtained while they were active. A packet may carry a fragment with a high recorded past yield, selected from the donor's **current** genome; the pointer control uses the fragment at the current instruction pointer. A shuffled-cargo control preserves that selected fragment's digit and ancestry multiset.
- A remembered forecast can orient its living host for three subsequent forage actions after the future environmental event is revealed; each oriented action costs 0.01 energy. Both learned and last-signal arms receive the same rule. The commitment still precedes the reveal and has no future input.
- A necrotic portfolio samples five archived code records and scores their past channel-specific forage against current local stock and the living population's channel coverage. Random, simple productivity-ranked, and portfolio-selected but order-shuffled controls retain the same archive and energy rules.

The first pass used 30 seeds 25001–25030. Six further development seeds 26001–26006 were inspected before this plan; outcome seeds 27001–27030 have not been examined. This is a locally fixed fresh-seed follow-up, not an independently witnessed preregistration. Parameter selection is informed by the exploratory results, and all claims remain about this implemented model. All research remains separate from Amelia and her live database.

## Shared setup

Thirty fresh populations warm for 30 model-time units, retaining the 29-gate graph, Θ = 12 flight, asynchronous clocks and finite three-channel environment. All subsequent branches share each root's complete state and RNG; retained material must fit the 512-digit cap before it is applied after the fork. For the living-ecology comparison, alternating sorted living IDs are killed as the same shock before branching, and each branch runs 180 further model-time units. The correlated environmental signal reverses direction at epoch 20; its private RNG is not read by the agents' forecast routine. Earlier commitments pending at the fork remain pending.

The seven living arms are: advanced (portfolio archive, learned forecast, experienced vectors, salvage, three-action orientation); pointer-cargo only; shuffled-cargo only; last-signal forecast only; no orientation only; no salvage only; and an earlier-policy control (random archive, last signal, no vectors or salvage, no orientation). Direct infection, ordinary splice, active corpse recycling, local-sum bets and the same upper limits remain in every branch. Comparisons include the intervention's effect on future population and opportunities; realized actions, costs and inputs can diverge.

For complete-loss recovery, all living agents are killed in each matched branch, then the archive policies are portfolio, random, productivity-ranked, portfolio with recalled digit order shuffled, and an emptied archive/corpse bank. Other mechanisms use advanced settings, the same eight-host recovery floor, same starting resources and same renewal rules. Archive shuffling occurs before the usual one-digit mutation and keeps digit counts and ancestry together. The empty bank retains the event record, but no recoverable archive material.

## Fixed contrasts

The following six paired-seed contrasts use 10,000 bootstrap resamples and Bonferroni 99.1667% percentile intervals (0.05 divided across six tests):

1. Living ecology: advanced minus pointer-cargo control, total nutrient harvested.
2. Living ecology: advanced minus shuffled-cargo control, total nutrient harvested.
3. Complete-loss recovery: portfolio minus random archive, total nutrient harvested.
4. Complete-loss recovery: portfolio minus shuffled-portfolio archive, total nutrient harvested.
5. Living ecology: learned minus last-signal forecast, total nutrient harvested.
6. Living ecology: external reactor renewal in the no-salvage arm minus that in the advanced arm; positive means less external renewal when salvage is enabled.

Pre-specified secondary descriptions include agent-time, final living agents, extinctions, consumed versus proposed resources, archive births and ordinary fissions, total and action-normalized intake, recovered and released meltdown energy, vector infections and their per-seed mean intake at the next observed host action, number of eligible or missing observations, and learned versus last-signal hits over the same completed windows. The recipient-action analysis conditions on contact and survival and is not a randomized host-level trial. The advanced-versus-earlier-policy total effect and prediction-controlled environmental performance are exploratory. Intervals do not establish equivalence when they cross zero. All external and endogenous energy inputs are reported separately; lower external reactor renewal may reflect differing downstream demand.

Three selected fresh branches and their available logs will be independently regenerated, with code provenance and energy paths audited. SQLite restart and local simulated cloud-contract tests must pass. The supplied Supabase adapter remains untested against a live isolated project and never points at Amelia's project in this study.
