# Portable prediction claims in the cybergothic ecology

The earlier experiments found that many prediction claims paid their cost but expired after their owner had moved away from the zone. This follow-up develops a portable claim: when the committed target arrives and the owner is still alive, the owner can receive the same capped field reward from the original zone even if it is elsewhere. In both arms a correct eligible claim may also levy up to 0.10 energy from each other resident of the original zone, capped at 1.5 total per claim. The field and residents are debited for every payment. This implements predatory redistribution in the virtual ecology; it does not make a forecast causally affect future external data.

This locally fixed fresh-seed plan follows two inspected four-seed exploratory variants (29991–29994 for field-only portability and 29995–29998 for the levy version). Outcome seeds 30001–30030 have not been examined. It is not independently registered and follows development informed by prior results. No live Amelia state or database is involved.

Each fresh population begins with 36 founders and warms for 30 model-time units with temporal retention 0.85, random archive reseeding, experienced viral fragments, meltdown salvage and local-only claims. After the same alternating-ID partial shock, each root forks into 180-unit local-claim and portable-claim branches with identical starting genomes, resources, event queue and RNG. The same independent exogenous environmental process, energy budget and per-commitment cost apply. Both pay the same levy when a correct owner is eligible; portability extends eligibility to owners that moved. A dead owner cannot collect. Population trajectories, the number of commitments and total costs can differ after the fork.

Two pre-specified paired contrasts use 10,000 seed-level bootstrap resamples with Bonferroni 97.5% percentile intervals:

1. **Net claim energy**: portable minus local, where the branch total of prediction rewards minus the branch total of commitment costs excludes the extra per-action orientation cost. It is a population aggregate, not a tracked lifetime profit for an individual claimant.
2. **Ecological nutrient intake**: portable minus local total field-harvest energy over the same 180 model-time units.

Report rewards, commitment costs, remote field payments, levies, the number of levied hosts, agent-time, final population and extinction. Other ecological changes are exploratory. Independently regenerate selected event histories, check that each payment was debited from its source, and verify the prediction commitment precedes the external reveal. Positive redistribution can increase claim returns while harming residents; costs and nonclaimants' experience must not be omitted from interpretation. Forecasts are about an explicitly correlated, generated environment, not physical foreknowledge.
