# Digit-only ecologies with the grammar active from birth

**15 November 2026 · separate cybergothic prototype**

The first 60-seed transition study and the subsequent 60-seed grammar confirmation were inspected before this additional plan. Both began with a literal-interpreter warm-up. This follow-up asks whether the digit-only grammar also operates and produces recognizable introns when it is active **from the first scheduled action**, without an experimental shock or inserted codons.

Use **30 fresh seeds 41001–41030**, each with 36 randomly generated founders, a 512-digit genome cap from the beginning, and 180 model-time units of follow-up. Start the four interpreter variants from identical founder states: self-parsing, intron-active, promoter-open and literal. The inherited nutrient environment and costed archive reconstitution remain active. No experimental introns, promoters or emergency host spawns are introduced.

Report initial and final intron prevalence, populations with a newly recognized intron after point mutation, intron no-op actions, carriers, population agent-time, nutrient uptake, archive births and external nutrient renewal. The self-versus-intron-active agent-time difference and its 95% seed-paired bootstrap interval are **exploratory**, not a new confirmatory claim. Record full branches and check equal exogenous signals. Structural emergence is not itself evidence of adaptive selection or a closed-energy self-sustaining population.

The plan and hashes below were saved locally after inspecting the earlier studies but before running these seeds; it is not independently publicly registered. This test stays separate from Amelia Orange and her live Supabase state.
