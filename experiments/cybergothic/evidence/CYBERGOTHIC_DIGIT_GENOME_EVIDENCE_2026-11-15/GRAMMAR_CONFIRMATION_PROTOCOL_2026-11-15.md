# Fresh confirmation: self-parsing versus literal population activity

**15 November 2026 · separate virtual cybergothic ecology**

The first digit-genome study, seeds 39001–39060, had been inspected before this plan. Its two planned self-parser versus intron-active outcomes did not clearly separate. A secondary comparison found higher mean agent-time under the full self-parser than under the literal interpreter. This follow-up tests that newly selected whole-grammar contrast on fresh populations; it does not retroactively make the earlier secondary analysis primary.

Run the exact frozen `digit_study.py` and four arms from the first plan for **60 uninspected seeds 40001–40060**, with identical warm-up, shock, genome cap and 120-unit follow-up. The single primary outcome is **self-parsing minus literal population agent-time**, computed per matched seed from the birth/death event ledger. Use 10,000 seed-paired bootstrap resamples and a two-sided **95%** percentile interval. Nutrient uptake, final population, meltdowns and archive births are secondary, unadjusted descriptions.

This compares the whole experimental grammar—including promoter regulation, regulatory codons and intron no-ops—with ordinary literal execution. It cannot isolate the effect of introns or demonstrate selection for them. The existing archive reactor and external nutrient renewal remain active. No Amelia Orange or live Supabase state is involved. This plan and the following hashes were saved locally before the fresh runs; this is not an independently witnessed public preregistration.
