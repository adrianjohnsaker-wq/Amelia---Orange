# Larger fresh replication: intact viral-stop recipients

**Plan fixed locally after inspecting both preceding cohorts and before this cohort · 15 November 2026**

The first 120-population study produced exploratory survival differences favoring intact introns. A first fresh confirmation found differences in the same direction among recipients of the real viral insertion, but its multiplicity-adjusted 97.5% intervals touched or crossed zero. The earlier transfer-by-stop interactions were also inconclusive. These results were examined before choosing to run this larger follow-up.

Using the unchanged six-arm `stop_trial.py` and eligibility conditions from the original protocol, screen **300 new independent populations with seeds 44001–44300**. Follow every qualifying natural infection in all six branches for 24 model-time units. Include only the first qualifying infection by time 150 per population, report every noneligible population, and make no outcome-based exclusions or sample extensions.

The two prespecified primary contrasts are the population-paired differences in 24-unit survival: (1) intact transfer minus physically ruptured-stop transfer, and (2) intact transfer minus recipient-only active-interpreter transfer. Compute 10,000 seed-paired bootstrap resamples and two-sided **97.5% percentile intervals**. The controls and their interpretive limitations remain those documented in the preceding confirmation plan: the physical edit changes a digit, and the interpreter control changes expression of all introns in the selected recipient.

Secondary, unadjusted 95% analyses include the two corresponding comparisons under sham exposure; transfer-by-stop and transfer-by-interpreter survival interactions; transfer-by-stop next-action nutrient interaction; no-op frequency, recipient nutrient uptake, offspring and subsequent lineage transmissions. Inventory natural direct/vector boundary splits separately. These outcomes do not establish that the inserted viral payload alone caused any host-survival difference, or that selection favored an intron.

Replay all branches and independently audit selected ledgers. This is an additional fresh cohort chosen after two earlier results; it is not an independently witnessed public preregistration and should not be presented as though there had been no previous looks. All runs retain external nutrient renewal and the costed archive; no Amelia Orange state is touched.
