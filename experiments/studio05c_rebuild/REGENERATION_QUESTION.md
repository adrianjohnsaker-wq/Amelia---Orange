# Does the learner–house pair regenerate its territory? (written before the run, 26 October 2026)

**Setup.** This runs on the Studio 05c rebuild (`s05c.mjs`), only if that rebuild first reproduces the 05c pattern. For each of the 8 worlds, start from the converged d = 3 state after 20 seasons and run 6 more seasons with one of four interventions, applied to every lineage's house at the fork:

| Arm | Intervention | Occupants |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | none (control) | learner |
| R1 | erase the house record of 2→7 (S = D = 0) | learner |
| R2 | erase the whole detour 1→2, 2→7, 7→8 | learner |
| R3 | erase the house record of 2→7 | unchanged Amelias (no V read or written) |

**Regenerated (per world, last of the 6 seasons):** mean house pull on 2→7 ≥ 0.5, and no deaths, and mean routes ≥ 6.5.

**Answer for R1, the main question.**
- **Yes, the pair rebuilds its territory:** regenerated in ≥ 6 of 8 worlds.
- **No:** 2 or fewer.
- **Conditional on the world:** in between.

R0, R2 and R3 are reported with the same rule.
