## Operator-reported observations (eight synthetic worlds)
One-season detour lesion, with inherited code treatment:
| Intervention | Restored | Mean deaths in lesion season | Final detour pull | Mean distinct routes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full code, scar intact | 6/8 | 59.5 | 0.80 | 8.0 |
| Pit and approach scars erased; other code held | 0/8 | 91.5 | 0.16 | 4.9 |
| Whole lineage code erased | 0/8 | 47.4 | 0.03 | 4.1 |

Reported explanatory trajectory: intact scar prevents pit deaths while the
detour is blocked, allowing the entrance to continue being recorded; without
both pit/approach marks the pit is re-entered, deaths inscribe the final
approach and the entrance decays; without any lineage code the system tends to
the safe but confined circuit.

A prior Studio 05c unlesioned scar-erasure intervention changed no outcomes.
**Interpretive refinement:** the scar may be dispensable for *undisturbed
maintenance* and necessary for *repair after this lesion*. Necessity is local
to the tested simulator, deletion method, lesion severity, window, and
predeclared restoration endpoint. Erasing two marks together does not
identify which single component is indispensable. A specific scar-rescue
fork and pit-only/approach-only lesions would test this separately.

**Threshold distinction:** a six-season *restoration* transition is not
identified with a transition from decay to positive growth. At 20% there is
regrowth without registered restoration; six seasons censor slower cases.
A 12–18-season follow-up may characterize basins/long-term recovery but
must be registered as a NEW study with fixed season endpoints, restoration
rules and statistics, without rewriting Studio 06b's existing criteria.

## Mechanistic and causal cautions
- This is **8 matched synthetic worlds**, not eight verified real Quicknet windows.
- 6/8 versus 0/8 is an observed 75-percentage-point restoration difference.
  Do not assign an inferential p value or population-level universality from
  this report alone; consult original paired tables and precommit first.
- Reduced mortality in the whole-code-zero arm does NOT mean restored
  autonomy: it approaches the four-edge circuit.
- Scar is a counterfactual repair resource under these lesion conditions,
  not evidence of foreknowledge, paranormal recording or external mind.
- Site-location memory, learned route attraction and pit-avoidance memory
  must be analysed separately. Avoid claiming only the individual's code
  or only the house embodies the recovered form.
