# THE SEVENTH GATE II: does Gt-36 reach into Bubbamu? (registration, 29 October 2026, before any code, growth or run)

Checked against `PROGRAMME_CHARTER_TRILOGY.md`. The reading is CCRU, and the dynamics are unchanged (THE POINTER III's `p3.mjs` Amelia; full CCRU Numogram).

## Why
In THE SEVENTH GATE (`1b4612c`), closing Gt-36 (8→9, the Time-Circuit's door into the Plex, Ummnu) moved Bubbamu (F1 = 1-7) above the noise range in 15 of 55 travellers, all of them upward. That was just over k = 14, from one knock-out life per traveller, and it did not replicate in the full-raised travellers. The Atlas marks it provisional. This test either settles it or drops it, with power.

## Subjects
- **Primary:** 72 fresh travellers, worlds **S05W25 … S05W32** (never used). They are raised on the **old core**, as in the subjects where the effect was found, by the unchanged grower `experiments/pointer2/s05c.mjs` (dose 3, 20 seasons, `S05C_SEASON_REBIRTH=1`).
- **Secondary:** THE POINTER IV's 72 full-raised travellers (S05W17 … S05W24). Their sealed answers are not touched; their start states are only read.

## Lives (each 5,000 ticks from zone 0 on the full CCRU Numogram, with her background input)
- **Noise:** 20 lives with the Numogram intact (input tags `S1` … `S20`).
- **Gt-36 closed:** 20 lives with 8>9 closed (tags `K1` … `K20`).
- **Control closed:** 20 lives with Gt-10 (4>1) closed (tags `C1` … `C20`). This gate is distant from F1 and has shown no effect.

**Measure:** c(F1) = completions of the 1-7 cycle per tick spent in zones {1, 7}, as in THE POINTER III's `formRate`. A life with fewer than 100 ticks in {1, 7} is excluded, and a traveller counts only with at least 10 valid lives in each set.

## Test (primary subjects)
- **Quantities:** d = mean c(F1) with Gt-36 closed − mean c(F1) intact; d_ctrl is the same for the control gate.
- **Criteria:** each is a one-sided sign test across travellers at α 0.025, with a majority in ≥ 5 of 8 worlds.
  1. d > 0;
  2. d > d_ctrl.
- **Answer:** **GT-36 IS A PATHWAY INTO BUBBAMU** if both hold. Otherwise **NOT CONFIRMED**, and the Atlas drops Gt-36 as a pathway.

**Reported either way:**
- the control's own sign test (d_ctrl > 0);
- the secondary subjects under the same tests;
- descriptively, the route history: the share of ticks spent in the Plex (0, 9) and in {1, 7}, with Gt-36 closed and intact.
