How this model reads the outline

The circulation runs Air → Fire → Earth. The Time-Circuit is zones 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, and its currents close the loop 5::4 → 1, 8::1 → 7, 7::2 → 5. Under the bridge, homeostasis lives in Air, Fire and Earth.

Water is the Warp, and the Union is the Plex. The Warp's current (6 − 3 = 3) feeds itself, a vortex rather than a circuit, and the Plex's current (9) feeds itself. These two sectors are the Outside.

The demon classes follow the regions. Chronodemons have both zones in the Time-Circuit, Xenodemons both outside, and Amphidemons bridge them. This is the CCRU's own split, not "2–7 against 0, 1, 8, 9".

The doors. Air opens onto Spirit through Gt-36 (8 → 9). Fire opens into Water through Gt-3 (2 → 3), and Earth through Gt-15 (5 → 6). The outline's example, Earth 4 → its partner 5 → up into Water 6, is exactly canonical: a syzygy, then Gt-15.

The Outside is one-way. No canonical move leads from Water or the Union back into the Time-Circuit. So step 4 reads backward: from the Union along the route that brought form to Spirit (for example 4 → 1 → 8 → 9), which is the only Union-to-Earth reading the lattice allows.

Letters. Only certain letters are shown: the Tablet of Union, each Watchtower's three Holy Names across its Great Cross, and its King. The remaining 576 squares await a verified transcription. Addresses, roles and routing do not need them; name extraction by Dee's paths does.

The default Call is the opening line of the First Call. Its first four words reduce under A1Z26 to 9 (OL 27, SONF 54, VORSG 81, GOHO 45). Entering at 8, the first word opens Gt-36 into the Union; entering at 1, there is no canonical move to 9 and the Call circulates in time.
