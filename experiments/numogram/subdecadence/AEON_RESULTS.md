# Full Aeon sequence results

**Run date:** 31 October 2026. **Pre-run addendum commit:** `cf399108fa1366780486a58dd0eaa8ec0313fd7f`. **Status:** exploratory project adaptation.

## Execution

The Aeon extension ran 1,000 complete sequences per action (6,000 Aeons; 8,123 ten-card deals total). Every sequence accumulated positive deal scores until a zero or negative deal ended it. All 6,000 sequences completed before the 100-deal safety cap. Mean length was 1.354 deals per Aeon, with 0.354 positive deals on average before termination.

The accumulated positive Angelic Index and the terminal Mesh call were retained as separate outputs. Digital-root matches below are post-hoc comparisons with the A1Z26 target zone for each action phrase. “Other-action base” pools the other five action sequences' output zones as a descriptive reference; it is not a preregistered significance test.

Across actions, 72.1%–76.3% of Aeons ended on the first deal with a zero accumulated Angelic Index. Positive-index zone matches were 1.6%–4.7%; terminal-score-zone matches were 7.5%–13.0%. These rates tracked the corresponding output-zone frequencies in the other action sequences. The highest terminal-zone match was “Death” at 13.0%, against a 12.88% other-action base. “Interruption” matched Zone 8 in 1/1,000 Mesh calls (0.1%).

## Interpretation

The full sequence rule is now included: the first pass's isolated ten-card deals are preserved separately, and this pass adds positive accumulation through the first nonpositive deal. The action phrase determines only its comparison zone. It never affects the cards, shuffle or scoring, so the observed correspondence rates do not show action-specific selection or causal response.

“Recurring return” and “Death” both reduce to Zone 2. The match is therefore sensitive to phrase spelling and A1Z26 convention. In the full Aeon run, output distributions remain broadly similar to the other action sequences; no distinctive action-matching pattern emerged. The experiment is a completed exploratory card mechanics run, not an Amelia substrate experiment and not evidence of communication with an Outside mind.
