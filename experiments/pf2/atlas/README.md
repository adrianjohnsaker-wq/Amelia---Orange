# PF-2 Capture & Selection Atlas — exploratory, read-only

**Established 2026-10-23. Status:** independent *analysis branch*, NOT a scientific registration and NOT a modification to the signed PF-2 trial.

## Governing sequence

1. **Validate existing influence.** Distinguish correlation (cue-following), score contribution (inherited V), exact same-state selector-level causal ablation, and realized first-divergence.
2. **Map selection.** Describe which cue classes, lineages, routes and carried edge dispositions persist, recur, or disappear. Preserve the spiral of *differentiated recurrence* as a descriptive construct; duration alone does not count.
3. **Discriminate field-organising agency in a separate future experiment.** Only after (1)-(2) and explicit fresh registration: alternative inflow, graph rewiring, place-memory, lineage-swap, sham and reset controls; prospective unseen beacons. Do not infer agency from a named Pandemonium pattern or from unconditional cue-following.

## Frozen boundaries

- **Read-only:** never write to \`experiments/pf2/live/\`, the registered receiver, core Numogram, C1 or PF-1.
- P2-1 and P2-2 remain sealed; no inferential early results, interim stopping or threshold updates.
- The archived input may continue growing; every atlas report binds a finite **prefix tick count, last chained digest, and source commit**, rather than treating a moving prefix as a fixed trial.
- The script neither downloads a new beacon, acquires a lease, communicates outside, nor sends any notification.
- All reported source-verified rows preserve timestamp, round, randomness/signature, event digest, full per-life path, selected exit, V and reconstructed score components. Output is **derived** and **not a substitute** for RFC3161/BLS source verification.
- PF-2 controls are identical-input learner versus reborn unchanged, plus eight **domain-separated alternatives from the same physical beacon**. These are not independent physical beacon sources and do not constitute graph-rewired or place-only interventions.

## Exact local selector attribution

At each step, instrument only the *already-running deterministic replay's* trait-evolution call. Capture **post-evolution / pre-choice** open-gate scores using the frozen Numogram selector expression in canonical gate order. Four same-state analytic selectors:

| Counterfactual | Cue increment | Personal inherited V increment |
|---|---|---|
| actual | yes | yes |
| cueOff | no | yes |
| vOff | yes | no |
| bothOff | no | no |

- \`cuePivotal\`: \`actual.to !== cueOff.to\`.
- \`vPivotal\`: \`actual.to !== vOff.to\`.
- Report \`bothOff\` separately for interactions. Pivotal categories can overlap and need not sum to 100%.
- These are **local selector** interventions holding the evolved gate/zone state, PFM bias and prior history fixed. They are not whole-trajectory ablations. In particular, V ablation is not ablation of canonical PFM or all memory.
- A followed cue is not necessarily cue-pivotal; a nonzero V is not necessarily V-pivotal.
- Compare reconstructed actual to archived realized selection **at every choice**, and require exact archived-event and final-engine replay.
- A final cryptographic provenance claim requires successful independent \`PF2_LIVE_CHECK.py\` + \`PF2_REPLAY.mjs\` on that same prefix.

## Experimental philosophy

The objective is to measure **where** inflow captures transitions, **when** inherited memory redirects them, and **which** dispositions survive or proliferate, before testing whether external patterns themselves maintain and reproduce an organising power. The spiral of differentiated recurrence, not survival alone, is the developmental target.
