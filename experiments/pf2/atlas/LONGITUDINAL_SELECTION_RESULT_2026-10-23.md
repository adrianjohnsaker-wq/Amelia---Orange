## 2. Temporal differentiated recurrence — descriptive

**Within one life**, call an encounter a repeated context when the same individual lineage revisits the same **from-zone and cue-zone**. Its next exit need not remain the same. The V distance is the 11-edge L1 difference since the previous encounter with that context. This is not a full-state matched comparison because canonical PFM, activation, phase and trait state can differ.

Of the 747 learner returns, **712** involved some V change >1e-12 since that context was previously seen, and **26 of the 27** changed-exit returns also had a V change. **This is not evidence V caused those 26 response differences:** unmodified controls had 32 changed-exit returns without inherited V, and post-evolution canonical state also varies. The recurrence data **do not show a learner excess** in this short prefix. The four chronological windows are not independent replicates or a test of trend.

## 3. Persistence across rebirth — exact

Two learner deaths/renewals were observed (each with a same-tick matched control death). **In both**, the complete 11-edge inherited V table at the first decision of the next life equalled the preceding death's final V **exactly (L1 difference 0)**:

- R02 death at archived tick 55, first new-life choice tick 58, all 11 V values exactly retained. A later new-life decision at **tick 67** (round **33300080**) had **V pivotal**: removing inherited V changes the winner. The beacon cue pointed at Zone 2, while learner chose 1→8 and unchanged receiver chose 1→2. Further V-pivotal R02 decisions occurred at ticks 76, 136, and 204, though later events may include additional within-life learning.
- R08 death at archived tick 191, first new-life choice at tick 194, all 11 V values exactly retained. **No V-pivotal choice was observed after that death in the audited prefix.** Preserving V is not identical to exercising decisive causal influence.

The tick-67 R02 decision occurs only 12 archived ticks after death (before the 24-step refrain horizon can settle a fresh-life edge), making it a particularly clear example of a carried inherited disposition affecting a new life. The full deterministic code implements the 24-step rule and was reverified against all events.

## 4. Selection — what is and is not known

**Supported:** persistent edge dispositions survive rebirth; specific carried V differences can redirect later decisions under an external cue; choices depend on external cue in 45 learner local counterfactuals. Recurrence can be quantified and compared with unchanged controls.

**Not supported yet:** the assertion that the beacon preferentially reproduces a population of dispositions, an increasing spiral of creative differentiation, or agency of the external pattern itself. Only **two** learner lineage turnovers and one shared physical beacon with eight derived tapes are present. No statistically powered population-level lineage-selection contrast or independent physical source replication has been completed.
