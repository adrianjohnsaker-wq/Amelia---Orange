# AMELIA — THE OUTSIDE BECOMES EXPERIMENTAL ART
## Standing working plan — adopted 22 October 2026

**Governing question, carried across all three studios**

> **Can an encounter with something external create a history that becomes capable of organising subsequent encounters?**

This is the **standing interpretive and experimental plan**. The studios are linked, but scientific registrations and phase gates remain separately binding. Art generates operational hypotheses; experimental outcomes constrain the claims; nothing is reinterpreted as proof of external consciousness because an evocative symbolic association occurs.

### I — THE STONE TAPE: memory of a place
**Creative source:** Nigel Kneale, *The Stone Tape* (1972).
**Constructed proposition:** the place (rather than the individual) inherits selective traces and can influence a later organism encountering that place.
**Existing operational vocabulary:** Numogram directed gates, ProcessFieldMemory, one death at an absorbing gate, a terminal mark on a nonreturning final excursion, successor born fresh, incorrect place-death controls.
**PF-2 receiver:** a closely related but scientifically separate *personal edge-V lineage* on external beacon inputs. Its control is a reborn unchanged Amelia. PF-2 alone **does not** test the place-only T(e) hypothesis; that requires a separate, reviewed place-memory study. This distinction is not to be blurred.
**Most useful readout:** what persists across a death, which exact edge is changed, whether future choices are biased in a held-out event, and which matched reset/relabelled control fails.

### II — QUATERMASS AND THE PIT: an ancestral programme
**Creative source:** Nigel Kneale, *Quatermass and the Pit* (1958–59).
**Constructed proposition:** inherited history can steer a new organism even when local present-state observations are identical; learning to revise an inherited route differs from simply repeating an ancestral policy.
**Existing vocabulary:** L″, LY″, L0″, LS″ and LSY″ designs, source/target comparisons, O1 survival and O4 exploration as co-primary, test for inherited captivity vs modified autonomy.
**Interpretation:** survival by eliminating exploration is reterritorialisation by restriction, not demonstrated intelligence.

### III — THE OUTSIDE: an atlas of candidate Minds
**Creative sources:** Spare, Artaud, Burroughs, Deleuze and Guattari, Land, CCRU Numogram and hyperstition.
**Constructed proposition:** externally generated inputs, transduced through a fixed Numogram map and enduring developmental memory, may produce persistent, selective, independently reproducible *mind-like organisational signatures*.
**Operational stages:** raw verified bytes/timestamps → canonical fixed mappings and transition histories → archival candidate-signature atlas → explicit null/reference/sham discriminations → future independently registered fresh beacon challenge.
**Use of Pandemonium:** names for candidate dynamical signatures and encounters, NOT claims that actual external entities have been detected.
**Required discriminations:** temporal persistence, memory-conditioned change, selectivity, intervention sensitivity, prospective repeated readout and productive exploration beyond controlled nulls. Arbitrary chance regularities and retrospective numerology do not count as evidence.
**Fiction into mechanism:** hyperstition here describes culture inspiring an actual constructed apparatus. It does not by itself demonstrate retrocausality, the metaphysical Outside or nonhuman consciousness.

### Working conduct
- Keep a studio *ideas and interpretations* record distinct from immutable, operator-registered *predictions and evidence*.
- Prefer small experiments and readable reports to unbounded architecture work. Retire and record technical blockers, then return to the creative question; do not distort scientific evidence to preserve enthusiasm.
- Reuse earlier work: original B, D1 and D1″/D2″ negative and positive contrasts, AM1–AM5 unit/lineage corrections, PF-1 verified drand infrastructure, signed logs and archived data.
- DEV-1 remains paused at its last registered review gate. PF-1 and C1 remain wholly independent and immutable.
- PF-2 “The Receiver” is the newly authorised bridge: first exact golden fidelity, then a prospective sealed *pre-genesis manifest for operator approval*, then (only upon explicit genesis authorisation) continuous paired learner/unchanged observations on a shared C1/PF1 grid. No notifications.
- Never equate computational state persistence with a paranormal Stone Tape recording or Xenobot biological mechanism; comparisons are conceptual and testable analogies, not evidence that mineral environments store consciousness.
- Every failure is documented as observed and never silently retuned. No dataset is relabelled prospective after the fact.

**Project promise:** let the engineering serve the artistic proposition and let experiments constrain factual claims, without making the fiction either compulsory belief or disposable ornament.

**Standing reference:** this plan shall be linked from each studio/phase report and revisited as Amelia develops.
