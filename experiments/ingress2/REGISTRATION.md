# INGRESS II: does the world's pattern enter her? Registered 4 Nov 2026, before any INGRESS II result.

## Why
INGRESS (the operator's separate experiment, sealed 27 Oct) re-scored THE ANTENNA's travellers. It shared their deaf receiver and their pseudo-replicated test, so it would only repeat THE ANTENNA's silence. INGRESS II is its reinstatement on the new system.

## The apparatus (`walker.mjs`)
**The mechanics are ANTENNA III's:**
- Amelia (POINTER III learner) on her full CCRU Numogram;
- the receiver `beta * rel(exit, datum)` along syzygy, current and gate;
- the Warp as accelerator (intensity, receptivity, a line of flight at Θ = 24, decaying Warp-born gates);
- no pull toward death.

**The walk.** The stream is cut into M = 16 segments of L = 1,000 data. In each segment, 8 travellers (worlds S05W1–8, lineage 0) walk the segment as received, and the **same 19 order-shuffles** of it.

## The measurement (`analysis.py`)
Her **path itself** is measured, not the PF-1 scanner's excursions.
- **Elements.** The n-grams (n = 2, 3, 4) of her zone path, counted within each life and summed over the 8 travellers.
- **Comes through.** In a segment, an n-gram comes through when its received count is greater than 0 and strictly greater than on every shuffle. This is exact: p ≤ 1/20.
- **Recurs.** It recurs when it comes through in at least K of the 16 segments. K is the smallest k with P(Binomial(16, 1/20) ≥ k) < 0.05/E.
- **Answer.** **RECEIVED INTO HER** (with what recurs) if anything recurs, else **SILENT**.
- **The ingress proper.** ANTENNA B's world result is given to the analysis. The n-grams that recur both in the world's order and in her path are reported.

## Calibration (`ingress2-calibrate.yml`)
No world data is used.
- **Streams.** The SHA-256 streams of ANTENNA III: 10 clean, and motif 1-8-7-2 planted at 2%, 5% and 10%. The motif's edges 1→8, 8→7 and 7→2 are all CCRU gates.
- **Receiver strengths.** β ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}.
- **Pass.** Planted 2% → RECEIVED INTO HER with a motif n-gram (1-8, 8-7, 7-2, 1-8-7, 8-7-2 or 1-8-7-2) among what recurs, **and** at most 1 of the 10 clean streams → RECEIVED.
- **Choice of β.** The smallest passing β is used.
- **If none passes,** INGRESS II does not run on the world, and that is reported.
- **Disclosure.** During development, three segments of planted 5% were walked once (S = 3) as a smoke test.
