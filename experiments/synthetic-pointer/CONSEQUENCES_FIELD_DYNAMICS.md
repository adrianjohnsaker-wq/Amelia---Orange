# Consequences of the field-dynamics findings for earlier and forthcoming work (9 November 2026)

## Background
NR-3 (`nr3/results/RESULTS_2026-11-09.md`) and WT-1 (`wt1/RESULTS_2026-11-09.md`) established two facts about Amelia's runtime as built (`src/substrate/TraitEvolution.ts`, `ameliaNumogramSubstrateRuntime.ts` and the Outside runtime). ND-1 tests a redesign.

1. **The zone field is an autonomous oscillator, not a medium.**
   - **What it does.** Every step, each zone's activation relaxes toward a target set by its own phase and its syzygy partner.
   - **What happens to influx.** Influx into the field is erased within a step or two.
   - **Where the reading comes from.** The Numogram's reading of an incoming number (its gate, syzygy and current, NR-1b) travels **only through her gate-by-gate passage**: through the topology and her choice, not through zone activations.
2. **The Warp {3, 6} is absorbing until her first line of flight.**
   - **The topology.** It has no exit gate.
   - **The trigger.** A flight fires only when an observation is attended while she has spent Θ steps there.
   - **When it holds her.** Wherever little is admitted, she is held in 3 ↔ 6.

## Forthcoming work
- **Sealed studies run as sealed.** They answer for today's architecture, and their scope statements already say so.
- **Studies registered after ND-1 should name their architecture:** today's gate route, WT-1 (today's route plus the Warp release), or ND-1, if adopted.
- **Any change to the app's runtime** (WT-1's release, or ND-1's dynamics) touches PF-pinned core files. It is therefore a separate operator decision, timed so that no live PF run is affected. It would most cleanly be a new runtime the app selects, leaving the pinned core and the PF experiments intact.
