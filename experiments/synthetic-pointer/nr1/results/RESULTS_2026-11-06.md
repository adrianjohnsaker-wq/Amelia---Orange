# NR-1 NUMOGRAM READING, part 1 and NR-1b: results, 6 November 2026

**Order of work.**
1. **NR-1.** Registered (`../REGISTRATION.md`) in `bab4b4bc`, before any run.
2. **NR-1b.** Registered (`../REGISTRATION_1B.md`) in `df72172e`, after NR-1 and before its own data existed.

**Scope.** This is the repository architecture, with fresh runtimes and no body; it is not the tablet instance. No world data were used.

## Instrument
- **Runtime equivalence.** NRRuntime with the INGRESS receiver reproduced RepairedSyntheticRuntime exactly: 3 sequences × 2,000 steps.
- **Calibration: PASSED.**
  - **Positive control.** Adding a syzygy pull in the receiver raises syzygy readings, with p ≤ 0.01 in **10 of 10** clean streams.
  - **Null control.** Two independent isomorphic relabellings differ at p ≤ 0.05 in **2 of 30** tests (1.5 expected, at most 3 allowed).

## NR-1 primary result (registered measure, lags 1–4 pooled): 1 of 6 supported
- **Gate, CANONICAL vs REWIRED, is supported:** p = 2/4096, 12 of 12 units.
- **The other five are not** (p = 0.011 to 0.58).
- **Why the measure was weak.** The pooled measure was a poor choice. At lag 1 she goes to the number itself (identity +0.20), which by definition places her *away* from its syzygy, current and gate (−0.02 to −0.05). That lag-1 deficit cancels the reading one step later.

## NR-1b (confirmatory, lag 2, new held-out stream h02): **6 of 6 supported** (Holm, α = 0.05)

**What this establishes, in this architecture.**
- **The reading.** A number c that comes in is read by the Numogram in two moves. First she goes to c. Then the Numogram's own structure carries her on to c's **gate** (strongest), its **syzygy**, and its **current** (weakest; at the margin against the rewired graph).
- **What it isn't.** It does not come from the receiver, which here pulls only to c. It does not come from the graph's shape: the rewired graph with the same degrees does not do it. It does not come from the Numogram's form without its numbers: the same Numogram, with the numbers no longer matching its zones, does not do it.
- **The point.** It is the Numogram's **numeric** structure that reads the number. The reading is concentrated at the second step and has faded by the third.

## Descriptive

### The motif 1-8-7-2 (CANONICAL, IDENTITY receiver)
It is read back **as itself** after 62% (5%: 94 of 152) to 69% (10%: 209 of 302) of its occurrences. This is because 1-8-7-2 is a **native Numogram path**:
- 1 → 8 is the Murmur syzygy;
- 8 → 7 is the current of 8::1;
- 7 → 2 is the SubCrypt syzygy.

**Variant and controls.**
- **The chief variant is 7872.** The 2 ↔ 7 syzygy echoes back before the motif runs.
- **REWIRED** reproduces it after 12–13% of occurrences.
- **RELABEL** never reproduces it (0). There the same digits become other forms (1171, 1871, 8724), because there the numbers name different zones.

### Her output in Numogram terms (h01, CANONICAL, IDENTITY)
- **Where she spends her time.** Time-Circuit 63%, Plex 21%, Warp 16%.
- **Syzygy passages.** Murmur 1::8 (1,305), BarkerLoop 3::6 (1,079), SubCrypt 2::7 (819), Abyssal 0::9 (12), Centroid 4::5 (3).
- **Other passages.** 1,675 current passages and 2,947 gate passages. The REWIRED graph gives 891 gate passages.
- **Lines of flight.** 26 lines of flight out of the Warp, over 12 units.

## Limits
- **Partly by definition.** This is the Numogram doing what its definition says it does, now shown to survive her full dynamics (memory, Governor, gates, Warp) and measured against controls. That is the necessary foundation, not a discovery about an outside.
- **Partly the analyst's choice.** The lag-2 statistic was chosen after seeing NR-1's descriptive profile, and confirmed on fresh data. The pooled NR-1 result stands as registered.
- **Small effects.** The effects are a few percentage points per step. They are consistent across units, and their size is not large.
