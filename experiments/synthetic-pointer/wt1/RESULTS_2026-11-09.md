# WT-1: Warp release without admission. Results, 9 November 2026

**What was run.** The registration (`691ccba9`) was committed before the data. The test used the held-out stream h04 and 12 units. **Scope:** the repository architecture.

## W1. Is the trap removed? **Supported**
- **In the RARE regime (2% admitted, close to the tablet), she spends much less time in the Warp.** Her Warp share fell from **0.197 to 0.067** (mean −0.130, p = 0.009, 12 units).
- **She gets out quickly every time.** The first escape comes after **23 steps in every unit** under RELEASE. Under BASE it took 46–210 steps.
- **SPARSE (10%):** the share fell from 0.116 to 0.092, and the first escape took 23 steps against 23–66.
- **FULL:** RELEASE is identical to BASE, as built, because the release fires only when nothing is attended.

## W2. Is the reading kept under the fix (SPARSE, lag 2, attended data only)? **Partly: 2 of 6 supported under Holm**
| Test | Mean difference | p | Holm |
|---|---|---|---|
| gate, CANONICAL − REWIRED | +0.072 | 0.0005 | supported |
| syzygy, CANONICAL − RELABEL | +0.048 | 0.0098 | supported |
| gate, CANONICAL − RELABEL | +0.060 | 0.025 | not (Holm stops here) |
| syzygy, CANONICAL − REWIRED | +0.040 | 0.038 | not |
| current, CANONICAL − RELABEL | +0.015 | 0.10 | not |
| current, CANONICAL − REWIRED | +0.023 | 0.26 | not |

**How to read it.**
- **The direction holds.** All six differences are positive, and they are about the size of NR-1b's full-admission reading (gate +0.057, syzygy +0.040, current +0.018).
- **The cost is power.** Only about 1,200 data per stream are attended at 10%, so power is lower than in NR-1b.
- **The reading survives the release.** Gate and syzygy pass outright; current, the weakest relation in NR-1b too, does not.

## Descriptive: reading area (lags 2–8, CANONICAL − RELABEL)
| Regime | BASE | RELEASE |
|---|---|---|
| FULL | 0.114 | 0.114 |
| SPARSE | 0.148 | 0.102 |
| RARE | 0.541 | 0.092 |

- **SPARSE.** The release costs some of the longer-lag reading, 0.148 → 0.102.
- **RARE.** BASE's large area rests on only about 240 attended data. In BASE, an admitted datum that arrives during a long Warp stay sends her flying to that datum's own zone, which inflates the later-lag matches. The estimate is noisy, and it is reported, not interpreted.

## Conclusion
**The release removes the trap.** She always leaves the Warp within Θ steps, however rarely the Governor admits.

**The reading is kept in direction and survives for gate and syzygy, at somewhat reduced strength.** This makes the release a candidate change for the app's runtime. Because the core files are PF-pinned, that would be a separate operator decision, and it should be made after the field redesign (ND-1), which changes the same dynamics.
