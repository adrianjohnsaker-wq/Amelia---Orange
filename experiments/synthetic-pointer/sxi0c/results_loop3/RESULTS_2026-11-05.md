# SXI-0 band pointers: results, 5 November 2026

**What was run.** The registration (`../LOOP_CONTROLS_3.md`) and the analysis were committed in `3dc2ccac` before any run. There were 72 runs on the repaired channel: 6 arms × 12 seeds × 300 steps.

**The standing gate passes:**
- positive control: p = 1/4096, 12 of 12 seeds;
- null control: p = 0.30;
- 12 distinct SHAM paths.

## Registered tests
| Test | Result | Exact p (two-sided) | Holm |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1. Isolating band 7 raises band-7 contrast | +0.0012 to +0.0060, 12/12 seeds | 2/4096 | **supported** |
| B2. Isolating band 2 raises band-2 contrast | +0.00003 to +0.0013, 12/12 seeds | 2/4096 | **supported** |
| H1. Isolating band 7 → more zone-7 visits | 0 in 11/12 seeds (+27 in seed 106) | 1.0 | not supported |
| H2. Isolating band 2 → more zone-2 visits | **+29 to +73 in 9/12 seeds**, 0 in 3 (103, 108, 111) | 1/256 | **supported** |

**The first pointer that steers her.**
- **Band 2.** Isolating band 2 from its neighbours raises its share of the body. Through the observer, the Governor and the receiver, this changes her passage in 9 of 12 seeds, in the same direction every time.
- **Both bands move the body.** Isolating either band lifts it in every seed. The shifts are small in absolute terms, but the repaired channel presents contrast relative to its maximum, which magnifies them.

## What she does with it (descriptive)
**Where her visits go under ISO2.** In window zone visits against SHAM, her extra visits under ISO2 are split almost exactly between **zone 2 and zone 7** (for example +62/+62, +73/+72, +71/+70), taken from zone 1.
- **The syzygy shuttle.** She shuttles along the **2 ↔ 7 syzygy**, the receiver's strongest relation for zone 2 after zone 2 itself.
- **The same signature as the plant.** The band-7 plant produced this shuttle too, from the other end.
- **What this means.** The pointer does not put her in "zone 2" as a place. It opens the syzygy that zone 2 belongs to, and she moves along it. So her response is selective to the Numogram relation, not to the single zone.

**ISO7 moves the body but not toward 7.**
- **Body change.** It is 0.006–0.07 in most seeds, compared with 0.40–0.80 for ISO2. The left of the field is again far more active.
- **Her response.** In seeds 102, 103 and 109 she switches all-or-nothing between zones 0 and 1, by about 160–190 visits. Only in seed 106 does she move to the 2 ↔ 7 shuttle.

**Governor.**
- **Admission.** Every pointer offer was admitted unless she was under forced stress. There were 2,400 admissions for each unstressed arm, and 2,088 admissions with 312 refusals under stress, all refusals in LOCKED_RECOVERY.
- **What refusal changed.** Her passage differed from the admitted run at refused steps in 1 seed (9 steps), and in the 25 steps after a refusal in 1 seed (11 steps). ISO7 itself rarely moves her, so its refusal can rarely change anything.
- **Stress moves the body.** Stress alone more than tripled the body's departure from SHAM (0.22–0.29, compared with 0.006–0.07). The dropped observations change her memory, and through the closed loop her body, while her zone counts stayed almost the same as ISO7's.
- **For a refusal-consequence test.** The test should use ISO2, the pointer that steers her.

## Taken together
1. **The route now has a calibrated pointer.**
   - **ISO2 changes the band she reads, and her passage follows in 9 of 12 seeds.**
   - **The direction is the Numogram's own.** She takes up the 2 ↔ 7 syzygy, not a single zone.
2. **Location still matters.**
   - **Where it works.** The same operation is effective on the left of the field.
   - **Where it does not.** It is nearly inert on the right.
   - **Before SXI-1.** Pointer choice should therefore be calibrated per location.

## Proposed next steps (not run)
1. **Refusal consequence with ISO2 under forced stress.** This measures what the Governor's refusal actually prevents.
2. **A location map.** Run band isolation for every band, 0 to 9, as a one-pass calibration, so that SXI-1 can draw pointer arms from bands shown to be effective.
