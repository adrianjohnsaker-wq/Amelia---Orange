## Descriptive (not decisive)
- **After withdrawal.**
  - **The trace stays.** After the plant stops (steps 250–299), PLANT still visits zone 7 12–17 more times than SHAM, in every seed.
  - **One likely cause.** Attended observations enter her memory, and that memory bias lasts about 48 events.
  - **Not separated yet.** This run cannot tell her memory from body hysteresis. Doing so is the open-loop and replay control that SXI-1/2 need, as recommended earlier.
- **Lines of flight.** There are 0–2 lines of flight per run, each creating one Warp-born gate. Once she has left the Warp she rarely returns. WITHHELD never flies, because a flight needs an attended observation.
- **Pointers.** POINTER_A and POINTER_B mostly leave her zone visits within a few counts of SHAM, with larger departures in seeds 106 and 110. A is not distinguishable from B (p = 0.66).
  - **Interpretation.** On this field, weakening ten couplings for 200 steps barely changes what the body shows her. The pointers need to be stronger, or placed in different regions, before SXI-1.
- **Replay.** A re-run of SHAM, seed 102, reproduced **all 602 records and every checkpoint SHA-256 exactly**. Ledger heads differ only through the ledger's own write-time fields (`utc`, `monotonicNs`), which record when it was written, as they should. The wall-clock snapshot timestamp, the source of the earlier non-reproducibility, is gone.

## What this does and does not show
- **What it shows.** The repaired channel is a working, checked instrument. A known, planted body organisation passes into her passage strongly and specifically. Ordinary body variation does not fake it. The seeds are real units.
- **What it does not show.** The plant is imposed, so this is a **calibration**, not a discovery. It is not evidence of an independently organised pattern, agency, or anything entering from outside.

## Before SXI-1 on this route (recommendations)
1. **Register the repaired runtime** (`repaired.ts`, β = 2, Θ = 24) as the SXI-1 interface, together with these two controls as its standing gate.
2. **Add the open-loop and replay controls,** so persistence can be attributed to the closed loop rather than to the body's physics or her memory alone.
3. **Strengthen or re-place the pointer arms** so that they measurably change the body.
4. **Keep the governance question open.** Admission is a registered rule here, not the Governor's general judgement. Whether the Governor should admit or refuse the pointer change itself is still the architecture's open gate.
