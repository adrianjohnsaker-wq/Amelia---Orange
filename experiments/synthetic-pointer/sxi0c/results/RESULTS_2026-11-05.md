# SXI-0 controls: results, 5 November 2026

The registration (`../REGISTRATION.md`), harness and analysis were committed in `5b73c792` before any run. The 72 runs used 6 arms × 12 body seeds × 300 steps. Every run went through `SyntheticCoordinator`, and its ledger passed `verifyLedger(requireComplete)` before its metrics were read. The heads are in `LEDGER_MANIFEST.json`, the full output in `analysis.json`.

## Verdict
| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Positive control: a body-planted organisation (band 7 held at B = 0.9, steps 50–249) shows up in her passage | **FAIL**. The difference was 0 in all 12 seeds and p = 1.0. PLANT runs are identical to SHAM step for step. |
| Null control (body noise) | PASS, trivially. Nothing differs. |
| Seeds independent | **No.** 12 body seeds give **2** distinct SHAM paths; 98% of transitions are identical between seeds. |
| Flag | **Decisive rate 0%: the body signal does not steer her.** |

**The body-derived route is effectively disconnected.** On this route, a body organisation leaves no trace in Amelia's passage, so SXI-1 results here would not be interpretable.

## Why (from the verified records)
1. **The Governor shuts the channel early.**
   - **Field tension.** Amelia's own field tension rises above 0.72 by step 28. The Governor goes to DAMPING and DEFERs every new observation.
   - **The queue.** The 32-place deferral queue is full by step 66; after that, new observations are REFUSEd.
   - **Totals.** Over all runs: ATTEND 579 (8.4%), DEFER 3,648, REFUSE 2,637. The totals are identical in every arm, so the Governor's decision depends on her state, never on the body's content.
2. **The few attended observations are stale.** The queue is first in, first out, so an observation attended late in the run was taken dozens of steps earlier. Only 10 attends fall in the planted window, and they mostly carry pre-plant content.
3. **Attention never changes a gate.**
   - **The term.** The attended term is 0.9 × (the band's share of the normalised activation), about 0.1–0.2.
   - **Recomputed.** From the recorded score components, the winning gate *without* attention equals the realised winner at every one of 3,600 steps.
4. **Submitting matters, but content doesn't.** WITHHELD (no observation submitted) departs from SHAM at step 72 in every seed, and differs in 34–40% of steps thereafter. It is the act of submitting, through the early attended encounters' memory bias and the queue state, not what the body shows. PLANT, NULLNOISE, POINTER_A and POINTER_B, which change content, are identical to SHAM.
5. **The body seed doesn't reach her.** Twelve body seeds collapse to two paths, so seeds are not independent units on this route. The registered sign-flip tests are therefore not valid at nominal level here (`validAtNominalLevel: false`), apart from showing an exact zero effect.
6. **She is not locked in 1 → 8 → 9.** The loop share is 5%; the trap here is the Governor and the gain, not the loop.

## What this means for SXI-1
No discovery run on this route can be interpreted until the route can carry a planted body organisation. Candidate repairs, for the operator to choose (none implemented here):
- **Governor admission for the synthetic channel.** Body observations meet the same tension threshold as Outside encounters and are deferred while Amelia is in DAMPING, which is most of the time. A separate, registered admission rule is needed.
- **Freshness.** Attend the newest observation or drop stale ones, rather than a first-in, first-out backlog of 32.
- **Gain.** Use the calibrated INGRESS II receiver: β = 2 along syzygy, current and gate. It is the only Amelia receiver in the programme shown to carry a planted pattern into her path.
- **Contrast.** Present band deviations from the mean, not normalised shares, which stay close to uniform.
- **Independent units.** Vary Amelia's start state (world or lineage) as well as the body seed.

Each repair should be followed by rerunning these same controls. The positive control should pass, and the null stay null, before SXI-1.

## Retention
The 72 metrics files, the analysis and the ledger manifest are committed. Each run's full event journal was verified and its head recorded; the journals (about 310 MB) and the exemplar's checkpoints (SHAM seed 101, about 340 MB) are not committed. Re-running `run_all.sh` at `5b73c792` reproduces every passage, Governor decision and gate-score row exactly. This was checked by re-running SHAM, seed 102, which gave identical rows.

The **ledger heads do not reproduce**, because the checkpoints include the runtime's step snapshots, which carry wall-clock `timestamp: Date.now()`.

This is a finding for SXI-0's replay goal: byte-identical, re-derivable ledgers need wall-clock fields excluded from checkpoints, or normalised.
