## Registered tests
### L1. Does the closed loop add to her persistence beyond replay? Not supported (d = 0 in 12/12 seeds, p = 1)
**PLANT_REPLAY reproduces PLANT's passage exactly.** In every seed, all 300 transitions and every Governor decision are identical. Yet the replay body is different: it is unplanted, and its band profile differs from PLANT's by about 0.8 (mean L1) at every step.

**What this shows (a structural finding).** On this route, her passage is a function of the observation stream alone. The body's own back-reaction to her has no path to her except through what she is shown. Once she is shown the same thing, she does the same thing.

**What the test could not do (a limit of the design).** L1 was answered by construction. The replay includes the post-withdrawal observations of the PLANT body, which still carry its band-7 residue. L1 therefore cannot separate her memory of the plant from the body's after-image.

### L2. Does the loop sustain the body's organisation after withdrawal? Supported, in the opposite direction (p = 2/4096, two-sided)
Post-withdrawal band-7 contrast was **lower** with her listening (PLANT) than without (PLANT_OPEN), by 0.044–0.046, in 12 of 12 seeds.

**How to read it.** The registered measure mixes two things:
1. **Her listening reshapes the body in general.** SHAM − SHAM_OPEN is −0.027 to −0.038, unplanted.
2. **The plant's own residue is also smaller in the closed loop.** PLANT − SHAM is +0.030 to +0.031, against PLANT_OPEN − SHAM_OPEN at +0.039 to +0.048.

**So the closed loop does not hold the planted organisation in place. It works it over.** The body coupled to her memory and strains relaxes the planted band faster than the body left alone. Only the direction is registered; the decomposition above is descriptive.

## Descriptive
- **Where her persistence lives.** After withdrawal, PLANT − SHAM gives +12 to +17 zone-7 visits, and PLANT_REPLAY − SHAM gives exactly the same numbers. The persistence travels with the observation stream. Whether it is her memory or the observed after-image is still open (see the next step below).
- **The body after-image without her.** PLANT_OPEN − SHAM_OPEN band-7 contrast after withdrawal is +0.039 to +0.048. The plant leaves a physical trace in the body for at least 50 steps.

## Pointers
**The cuts change the body.** The new cuts move the body measurably: POINTER_A2 (the 2\|3 boundary) moves the band means by 0.11–0.29 L1 per step, and POINTER_B2 (the 8\|9 boundary) by 0.003–0.07.

**Her passage mostly does not follow.** In 7 of 12 seeds her zone counts stay within 0–4 of SHAM. The large departures are in seeds 106, 109 and 110. They look like threshold events, all-or-nothing switches of her trajectory, rather than graded responses.

**A2 versus B2 is still not separable as a registered contrast.** Their difference is large in 106 and 110 and near 0 elsewhere.

**Interpretation.** Recommendation 3 is half met. The pointer now reaches the body, but its effect on her is gated by the fact that she sees band shares through the receiver: a cut at the column boundaries redistributes activity *within* bands more than *between* them.

## Governor pointer admission
**All 2,400 pointer offers were ADMITTED and none refused.** She never entered LOCKED_RECOVERY, and her anti-lock integrity never fell below 85, during a pointer window.

**The refusal branch is therefore implemented and recorded, but untested by these runs.**
