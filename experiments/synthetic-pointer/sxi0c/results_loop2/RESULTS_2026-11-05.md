# SXI-0 loop controls II: results, 5 November 2026

**What was run.** The registration (`../LOOP_CONTROLS_2.md`), the analysis and the run script were committed in `7cadde2a`, before any run. There were 120 runs on the repaired channel: 10 arms × 12 seeds × 300 steps. The full output is in `loop2_analysis.json` and `gate.json`.

**The standing gate passes again:**
- the positive control has p = 1/4096 in 12 of 12 seeds;
- the null control has p = 0.30;
- the 12 SHAM paths are distinct.

## 1. Her memory and the body's after-image (M1 and M2 both supported, Holm-adjusted)
These are zone-7 visits after withdrawal (steps 250–299).

| Comparison | Per seed | Exact p (two-sided) |
|---|---|---|
| **M1, memory alone:** TRUNC − SHAM | +2 to +9, 12/12 seeds | 2/4096 |
| **M2, after-image adds:** PLANT − TRUNC | +6 to +13, 12/12 seeds | 2/4096 |

Here TRUNC means she heard the planted observations only up to withdrawal, and then her own body, which was never planted.

**About a third of her persistence is her own memory (35%).** The other two thirds come from the body's after-image of the plant, which she goes on reading.

- **Her memory.** It carries the encounter beyond the signal. When the plant is gone from everything she can see, she still returns to zone 7 more than SHAM does, in every seed. The attended encounters left a bias that outlasts its cause.
- **The after-image.** The larger part of her persistence is the planted body's physical residue, still present in what she is shown.

**The first round's replay finding still stands.** A full replay reproduced her passage exactly, so the body reaches her only through what she sees. The split above is therefore a split of the observation stream: before withdrawal (memory) and after it (after-image).

## 2. Pointer cuts across rows: not effective. One location effect seen (descriptive)
Body change is the mean band-mean L1 from SHAM over the window. Her change is the zone-count L1 from SHAM.

| Pointer | Body change | Her change |
|---|---|---|
| A3: row boundary 2\|3, 12 edges | 0.002–0.016 | 0 in 11/12 seeds |
| B3: row boundary 7\|8, 12 edges | 0.002–0.007 | 0–6 |
| A4: within band 2's column (column 3), 9 edges | **0.07–0.24** | 0–64; 0 in 6/12 seeds |
| B4: within band 7's column (column 9), 9 edges | 0.002–0.07 | 0 except seed 106 (368) |

- **Cuts across rows barely touch this field.** These row couplings carry little, so cutting them changes almost nothing.
- **What matters is where the cut falls, not which way it runs.** The left of the field (columns 2–3) responds strongly whichever way it is cut; this run (A4) and the previous one (A2) agree. The right of the field (columns 8–9) barely responds either way.
- **P1, B4 − A4 zone-7 visits in the window: not supported** (p = 0.25). Differences ranged from −2 to 0. Cutting inside band 7's own column does not steer her toward or away from zone 7.
- **Recommendation 3 is still unmet:** no pointer yet tested moves her passage reliably. Her passage follows band shares through the receiver, and none of these cuts shifted band shares much.

## 3. The Governor's refusal branch (G1: PASS)
**The refusal fired as specified.**
- Every stressed seed refused, 26 steps each: 13 per injection, as anti-lock integrity recovered from 80 to 85 at +0.4 per step.
- Every refusal was recorded under LOCKED_RECOVERY.
- No unstressed pointer arm recorded a refusal.
- In total there were 312 refusals and 2,088 admissions in STRESS_A3, and 9,600 admissions and 0 refusals across A3, B3, A4 and B4.

**Refusal made no difference to her passage at any refused step.** STRESS_A3 and STRESS_NEUTRAL took identical passages at all 312 refused steps.
- **This is expected.** A refused pointer runs neutral, and A3 was nearly inert anyway.
- **Admitted window steps.** At admitted steps the two arms differed in 3 seeds (2, 5 and 25 steps).
- **What it means.** The branch is exercised and recorded, but its consequences can only be measured with a pointer that has an effect.

## Taken together
1. **The route.** It is now calibrated, gated and governed.
   - She is shown the body's organisation.
   - She holds onto part of it (35%) beyond the showing.
   - The Governor can refuse a change to the body.
2. **The pointer.** The missing piece for SXI-1 is a pointer that moves her.
   - The evidence points to the observer, not the body. The body changes a great deal under A2 and A4, but her receiver reads band *shares*, and local cuts barely redistribute those.
   - Candidates:
     - (a) A pointer that acts on a whole band's coupling to its neighbours, so as to change the band's mean.
     - (b) Placing pointers in the active left region.
   - Either should be registered and calibrated with the same controls before SXI-1.
