# Correction to the ND-1e reading (10 November 2026)

**The claim corrected.** `RESULTS_2026-11-09.md` said that 2% detection "is at the order test's detection floor for every
architecture". **That is wrong.**

**Instrument check** (`ideal.ts`, descriptive). A perfect reader, whose passage copies its input exactly, was run through the
same order harness on the same eight ND-1e 2% streams. It was **RECEIVED on 8 of 8**: 1872 recurs in 9–12 of 12 segments.
The test can see a 2% order.

**Where the signal is lost: in her passage, at the gates.** Over the 954 planted motifs in the ND-1e 2% streams:

| | D10 | Today's architecture |
|---|---|---|
| The zone she stands on when the motif begins has a gate to 1 | 72% | 71% |
| Exact 1-8-7-2 when that gate exists | **92%** | 91% |
| Exact 1-8-7-2 overall | 69% | 66% |

The rest are motifs that begin while she stands on 0, 2, 3 or 6. None of those has a gate to 1, so the first step cannot be
taken.

**What this means.**
- **Both architectures follow the input faithfully wherever the Numogram's gates allow.**
- **The loss is structural.** The rule "she can only move along an existing gate" removes about 30% of the order before any
  reading happens. At 2% this pushes the order below the detection threshold.
- **The field designs were not the limit.** The rule is.
