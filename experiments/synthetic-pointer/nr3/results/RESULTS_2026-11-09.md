## What this shows
1. **In this runtime the Numogram's reading of a number is carried by its gate topology,** that is, by her moving along syzygy, current and gate edges. The zone field does not carry it.
2. **The zone field (`TraitEvolution`) is not a medium for incoming order.**
   - **What drives it.** It is driven by its own phase oscillation and by syzygetic averaging, and it relaxes toward targets set by sin(phase) and its partner zone.
   - **What happens to influx.** Influx is absorbed within a step or two, and leaves no relational trace.
   - **What a heavier weight on it does.** Weighting it more heavily in her choice (COUPLED) makes her follow the field's **autonomous** attractor (zone 1), not the input.
3. **So the review's architecture, on *this* field, carries the Outside less well, not better.** Its intuition fails here because the field, as built, is an autonomous oscillator rather than a responsive morphogenetic medium.
