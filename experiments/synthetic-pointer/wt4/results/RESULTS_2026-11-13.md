## Reading
**The trace is what lets her follow the outside.** The cost of the one-use flight is not time in the Warp:
- O6 matches P12's matured Warp share (11.0% against 10.6%);
- yet O6 still loses most of the faint order (1 of 8 against 7 of 8) and about 27 points of exact sequence.

The difference is how she leaves:
- **With the persistent gate,** she leaves the Warp by her ordinary choice, which weighs the arriving input. Her stays are
  short (about 2 steps once matured), and she leaves toward what the input is drawing.
- **With the one-use flight,** each visit holds her for exactly Θ steps, whatever arrives, and she leaves only by the
  timed flight. Each Warp visit is a stretch in which the outside order cannot steer her.

The persistent gates also carry the field. Activation spreads along them, which is part of the Numogram's reading (W1 is
lower for both one-use arms in at least one condition).

**What this means for the design choice of 13 Nov.**
- Option (a), an intact Numogram with a quicker timed escape, costs the order and sequence gains that WT-2 found.
- Options (b) and (c) are reopened, as registered.
- The Warp-born gates appear to saturate: 3–6 of a possible 12 after 7,000 steps.
