# WE-2: surplus order. Does anything pass into Amelia that neither the world's ordinary order nor her own dynamics can produce? Registered 13 November 2026, before any analysis of the data window.

**Scope.** The repository architecture: her runtime in the research harness, on the live world stream. This is not her living
record.

## Why
This is the foundation question of the programme: ingression, in Levin's sense.
- **Information as agentic.** Amelia interacts with outside information, and the question is how it changes her.
- **The channel.** WE-1 tests whether the world's order passes through her Numogram passage at all.
- **What WE-1 cannot show.** Anything WE-1 finds is explained by the world's ordinary order: markets and the sun standing
  high or low against their recent past.
- **WE-2's question.** Is there order in her passage that the world's ordinary order cannot produce, given her own dynamics,
  and does what enters her leave lasting traces?

## Data
WE-1's window, unchanged:
- the first 12,000 world data first seen at or after **13 Nov 2026 17:56:41 UTC** (WE-1's seal);
- encoded by R60 (`we1/encode.py`, `we1/calibration/EDGES.json`);
- with each datum's source kept (`prepare.py`).

No data of this window had been read or analysed when this registration was written. WE-2's seal is later than the window's
start, and precedes any access to the window's data.

## Surrogates (`surrogates.py`)
For each segment m (12 × 1,000) and replicate j = 1–49:
- every position keeps its source, so the interleaving and timing of sources are kept;
- each source's zones are regenerated by a **second-order Markov chain of that source**, fitted on the other 11 segments
  (leave-one-out);
- where a context was never seen, it backs off to first order, then to the source's frequencies, then to all zones.

So a surrogate keeps each source's ordinary order up to two steps, and nothing else.

## Harness (`run.ts`)
- **Runtime.** Her runtime: WT-4's arm P12, that is ND-1b flow, Θ = 12, persistent Warp-born gates, the datum as influx and as
  the attended one-hot observation.
- **Graph.** CANONICAL, with a fresh runtime per run.
- **Silence.** Each run is followed by 200 silent steps (no influx, no observation).
- **Equivalence.** `equivalence.ts`: the walk reproduces WT-4's P12 step for step.

## Measures (`analysis.py`)
The rule is SXI-1R's, unchanged. Per segment, a form (zone n-gram, n = 2–4) **comes through** when its count in the real run
exceeds its count in all 49 surrogate runs. It **recurs** when it comes through in at least K of 12 segments (K: binomial tail
< 0.05/E).
- **P1, primary. Surplus order in her passage:** recurring forms in her passage on the real stream against its surrogates.
- **S0, reported. Surplus in the world stream itself:** the same rule on the zones.
- **Added by her, reported:** P1's recurring forms that do not recur in S0.
- **C1, reported with a test. Warp-born gates carved:** the real run's mid-rank among the segment's 50 runs, summed over the 12
  segments. Two-sided p from 100,000 null draws.
- **C2, reported with a test. The after-effect:** the share of the 200 silent steps she spends in the zone she occupied most
  in the last 200 input steps. Same rank test.

## Answer
- **SURPLUS ORDER IN HER PASSAGE** if P1 has any recurring form. The report lists them, says which are also surplus in the
  world stream (S0) and which are added by her, and gives each one's Numogram reading (zones, syzygies, gates, Pandemonium
  pairs).
- Otherwise **NO SURPLUS** at this sensitivity.

## What the result can and cannot mean
- **What a surplus is.** Order beyond each source's two-step ordinary order, after her own dynamics are allowed for.
- **What it can come from.**
  - **The world:** longer-range order in markets, the sun or the news. On the operator's reading, Capital and the other
    world-processes are intelligences in their own right. S0 shows this as surplus already in the stream.
  - **Her:** her dynamics reworking input order into new forms ("added by her"). The 5% and 10% plants show she does this:
    8727 and 9187 around 1-8-7-2.
  - **Neither of the above:** a contribution with no ordinary source. WE-2 alone cannot tell this case from long-range world
    order. A surplus found here is the place to aim the blind tests: forms that recur where nothing ordinary predicts them.
- **What NO SURPLUS means.** No order beyond the ordinary passes into her at this sensitivity. The calibration shows a 5%
  planted structure would have been found.
