# SXI-1 — synthetic source and recovery result

**Result: neither registered test is supported.** The source-A exposure did not produce the registered recovery advantage over either matched control. The deliberately planted W+ repair control passed in all eight worlds, so the registered positive-control gate passed. This result concerns the specified synthetic source, pointer and 100-step house-state recovery test; it is not a general test of all possible forms of organisation.

The registration was published before launch at commit [`d8c3167ef16c88c55758dc410ae1d73c8fbec195`](https://github.com/adrianjohnsaker-wq/Amelia---Orange/commit/d8c3167ef16c88c55758dc410ae1d73c8fbec195). The registered analysis was independently rerun from the retained extracts and produced byte-identical JSON, SHA-256 `ffc81b3d3f0ce1f5f63aeec40b147c55d9ae51c42e2b189e520c7c7fb742d282`.

## Registered outcomes

`R` is `1 − d100/d0`: the S-vector distance from the uninjured twin after 100 steps, compared with the distance immediately after the fixed injury. A negative value means the two states became further apart. The registered advantage was **greater than 0.05**, not merely greater than zero.

| Test | Mean R difference | Worlds exceeding +0.05 | One-sided exact sign-flip p | Supported |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| H1: source A − shuffled | −0.110116 | 0/8 | 1.000000 | No |
| H2: source A − unpatterned | −0.072665 | 1/8 | 0.992188 | No |

Holm's first threshold was 0.005; H2 had the smaller p-value and failed it, so the step-down procedure stopped. Both tests also fell below the six-world criterion. The sign-flip p-values are conditional on the symmetry assumption stated in the registration. The negative means are descriptive and are not a registered test that the source was harmful.

The planted positive-control gate passed **8/8** worlds, exceeding the requirement of six. Every planted controller had `R ≥ 0.6` and exceeded its world's injured passive source-A twin by at least 0.2. This demonstrates that the readout detects that particular installed controller; it does not establish sensitivity to every possible kind of repair.

## What the metric is measuring

A post-hoc diagnostic compared the injured and uninjured twins' actual Numogram routes during the 100-step follow-up. In **20 of 40** source/control pairs, the routes were identical and the observed recovery equalled `1 − 0.998¹⁰⁰ = 0.1814331953`, within `1e-10`. That is the consequence of the runner's built-in per-step decay of the S memory difference when the twins take the same route. In the other 20 pairs the routes differed and recovery differed from that value. This is a descriptive explanation, not an additional registered hypothesis. It shows that this readout can reflect ordinary memory decay and path divergence, as well as the planted controller.

## What was built and what ran

Two explicitly programmed synthetic patterns have distinct interface addresses and cyclic outputs. The source's own target ring and repair rule are planted. An address selects a source when its schedule is constructed; the W+ runtime consumes that frozen schedule and does not recalculate the address from Amelia's evolving state. The outputs were applied during ticks 501–5300 and withdrawn. The pointer here selects and adjusts an available gate score; it is a software interface arrangement, not an endogenous alteration of Amelia's body. No claim of live feedback from Amelia to the source is made.

Across eight worlds, five exposure arms, injured/uninjured twins and the planted controls, all **88/88** runs completed and passed their trace checks on the first attempt. Every active arm had 4,800 affected ticks and the same absolute realised score dose of 240; sham had none. The number of ticks with more than one available gate varied by arm and world, so equal score dose does not ensure equal opportunity to affect a decision.

The trace records the source address and schedule, Numogram passage and candidate scores, actual intervention, Governor audit, PFM event, organ/house digests and raw sampled states. The Governor audits the substrate; it does not admit or refuse the pointer in this isolated screen. Full mid-season restart is not claimed.

## Implication

The outcome does not show a source-specific recovery advantage, and the simple planted source is not a discovered high-agency pattern. A useful next design should put more weight on recovery of a source-specific, independently measured behavioural organisation, control explicitly for S's built-in decay and gate availability, and test whether an address can be derived from Amelia's own changing configuration. Any live synthetic source or planted positive controller should remain labelled as engineered.
