# SXI-2: live body-derived pointer — results

**Completed 5 November 2026. Both registered retention hypotheses are not supported.** Amelia's changing organ did select between two engineered synthetic sources, and her current state fed the selected source on every exposure step. The direct pointer manipulation affected realised Numogram passages. The study did not establish that the live feedback produced a distinctive retained route organisation after withdrawal.

The registration and executable analysis were published at commit [`7f860f0c4f75f5000c84014ca36ba749fce31d7b`](https://github.com/adrianjohnsaker-wq/Amelia---Orange/commit/7f860f0c4f75f5000c84014ca36ba749fce31d7b) before the fresh recipient and donor worlds were run. All 64 planned trajectories completed and passed the registered verifier: eight donor worlds and eight recipient worlds, each recipient with seven matched arms. There were no failed attempts. The eight recipients share the initial lineage and code; their world inputs differ. Results concern this isolated W+ fixture, not the deployed tablet app.

## Registered tests

The registered retention score R measures how much more the post-withdrawal three-destination motif distribution resembles the late-exposure window than the pre-exposure window. Each window contains 500 destinations and 498 motifs. The treatment was active during ticks 501–5300; the post-withdrawal window was 8001–8500. The registered criterion required an advantage above 0.05, Holm-adjusted p ≤ 0.01, and at least six of eight worlds above the margin.

| Comparison | Mean R difference | Worlds above +0.05 | One-sided p | Holm-adjusted p | Supported |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| H1: live minus donor replay | +0.270 | 5 / 8 | 0.1094 | 0.2188 | No |
| H2: live minus shuffled donor feedback | +0.058 | 4 / 8 | 0.4883 | 0.4883 | No |

The mean live-versus-replay contrast points in the predicted direction, but three of eight world differences are negative and the registered test is not met. The shuffled comparison also fails. The sign-flip p-values are conditional on independent, symmetric world-pair differences; distinct pseudorandom world namespaces sharing a lineage do not establish generalisation to a population of independently evolved bodies.

R is a temporal route-similarity measure, not recovery, task competence or a direct readout of a source's internal goal. A positive or negative score can arise from changing ordinary W+ dynamics. There was no whole-W+ positive control establishing that this measure would detect a retained target; the unit-test planted patterns verify calculation only. Thus the null limits this operational test and cannot exclude every possible form of retained organisation.

## What the live interface did

* During all **38,400 live exposure steps** across recipients, the selected source received the corresponding actual pre-step body feedback. The current organ-derived address selected source A for 20,018 steps and B for 18,382. Across full trajectories, the computed address changed between 113 and 2,760 times per world.
* At tick 700 an engineered organ-phase perturbation changed the used address in **8/8** dynamic-pointer recipients; the equal-magnitude orthogonal phase change retained the immediate address in **8/8**. In each targeted dynamic/frozen pair, the first 699 steps, mutated body, house, body feedback and source bank at tick 700 matched. The selected gate differed at that tick in **6/8** pairs. The realised destinations subsequently differed in **8/8** by tick 918; the earliest difference occurred at tick 700. This is evidence that the implemented pointer can change this fixture's trajectory, not evidence of a discovered autonomous mind.
* A one-step, same-body computational counterfactual replaced the live feedback with the independently recorded donor sample at each exposure step. It changed source state on **38,400/38,400** steps, the emitted symbol on **6,515/38,400**, and the selected gate on **5,321/38,400**. Among **33,106** steps with more than one gate, it changed the reconstructed winning destination on **294** (0.89%). These are counterfactual calculations from the observed live source state and gate scores, not separately realised future trajectories.
* The live, replay and shuffled arms each had 4,800 score modifications of −0.05 per recipient. Replay and shuffled used the **same donor feedback triples** in original and shuffled order. The live arm used its own actual body feedback, so its feedback multiset was not matched to the donor's. Gate availability and body histories can diverge after exposure. This design does not include a separate randomised-address control for the unaltered body.

The two source programs have explicitly programmed target states and update rules. Their use here tests a live synthetic interface, not a connection to an identified non-physical source. The Governor was audit-only; it did not admit or refuse the experimental input. Each trace records the address, feedback, source state, selected gate, available scores, passage and PFM chain, with native runtime state in a separately hashed sidecar.

## Verification and reproducibility

Every completed trace was independently reconstructed by the committed verifier. An additional post-run audit checked all **64** compressed trace hashes and decompressed hashes, arm identities, donor identities and input hashes. It independently recalculated the two contrasts and exact sign-flip p-values using integer motif counts and rational arithmetic. This is an independent calculation from the retained extracts, not an independent rerun of the simulations. The machine-readable records are `RESULTS.json`, `EXECUTION_RECEIPT.json`, `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.json` and `WORLD_ARCHIVE_HASHES.json` in this directory; the world evidence archives and their index contain the full traces, native sidecars and per-arm receipts.

The next useful step is a fresh, preregistered study with a whole-W+ positive control for retained target behaviour, a matched randomised or decoupled address control, and more independently initialised lineages. It should keep the real-time body-to-address-to-source loop and evaluate competence or target-specific persistence as well as route frequency. Such a study can assess a stronger form of organisation than this first route-retention screen.
