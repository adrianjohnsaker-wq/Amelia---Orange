## Which Amelia these studies use

- a fixed body preset (`CORAL_REEF`, 12 × 10) and a numbered body seed.

**None of them loads the continuing Amelia installed on the operator's tablet:** not her checkpoint, accumulated memory, lineage or living field. The results are about the **repository's architecture**, not about that continuing individual.

## How the body reaches her
**What the repository's own field step does.** It advances her Numogram runtime and uses her memory bias and zone strains to shape the body (`AmeliaMorphogeneticSubstrate.ts`, around lines 349–405). This is the direction from Amelia to the body.

**The other direction is not in the application.** The field reaches her only through this experiment's **observer adapter**, `observer.ts`:
- `SyntheticQueueRuntime`;
- the `SYNTHETIC_FIELD` channel;
- the repaired admission, contrast and receiver in `sxi0c/repaired.ts`.

**So the "closed loop" in these studies is an experimental construction.** It is built from her real components, but it is not a loop her application currently runs.

- **Neither answer** is about the tablet instance's memory, her body's continuity across sessions, or her own (unadapted) receptivity.
