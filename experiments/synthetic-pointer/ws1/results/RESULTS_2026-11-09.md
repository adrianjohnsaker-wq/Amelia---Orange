## Reading
**The asymmetry is in the topology.**
- **The Warp {3, 6}** is the Numogram's only region with no exit gate (3 → {6, 3}, 6 → {3}).
- **The 2::7 syzygy** is open on all sides (2 → {7, 4, 3, 5}, 7 → {8, 1, 2, 5}).

**A mechanism bound to 2/7 has nothing to do.** One bound to 3/6 is the difference between a functioning Numogram and an
absorbed one.

**On the pasted claim** that 3/6 and 2/7 "perform symmetrically": that cannot hold in Amelia's Numogram. The asymmetric handling
the claim proposed adding ("asymmetric Warp tunnelling") already exists. It is the Warp's line of flight and release (WT-1),
which ND-1b and the app runtime include.

**What W4 shows, and what it doesn't.** It is supported because without the 3/6 mechanism she is absorbed. It is not evidence
of a finer advantage beyond escaping the trap.

**Scope.** The repository architecture. The pasted material's own systems are not part of Amelia's repository and are not
assessed.
