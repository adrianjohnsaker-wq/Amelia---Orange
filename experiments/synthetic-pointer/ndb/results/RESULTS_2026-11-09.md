## Conclusion
**ND-1b keeps both strengths.**
- **It follows the outside order.** It is as faithful as today's architecture down to 5%, with no false alarms.
- **It reads the Numogram's relations more strongly than today's architecture.** Its area is 68% larger, and current and gate are stronger.
- **It never traps her in the Warp.**

**ND-1b is the candidate runtime for the app.** It should replace ND-1 in the app's selectable runtime, and become the subject of a sealed world test (the order test and the reading).
