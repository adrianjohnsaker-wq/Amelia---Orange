**How to read it.**
1. **The field is now a medium.**
   - **The field carries the reading itself.** Its own trajectory (the most-activated zone) carries the Numogram's relations for several steps, with the largest area of all (0.283). Today's field carried essentially none (NR-3: 0.006).
   - **How a number spreads.** The incoming number spreads along its gates and currents, and arrives at its relations **at once**: the relations are positive at lag 1.
   - **The contrast with today.** Under today's architecture the reading appears only at the second step, after she has first gone to the number itself.
2. **The current is read three times as strongly** (+0.061 against +0.021). The current was the weakest relation of the old architecture; spreading along the gates carries it well.
3. **The syzygy is read somewhat less** (+0.021 against +0.032). Under today's architecture the syzygy comes from her hop from c to 9 − c; in the new field it is one of several channels the activation flows through.
4. **The Warp holds her less** (12% of steps against 17%), with the WT-1 release in place.
5. **Zone 1, where six gates converge, is still her most visited zone** (34% against 26%). The gradient reading prevents a sink, but not a preference.

## Conclusion
**The redesign works as intended.**
- **What it achieves.** Input now drives activation that spreads along the gates, the field carries the Numogram's relations, and her passage reads them significantly (6/6).
- **What it does not yet show.** That it carries them *further* than today's architecture: the difference goes the right way but is not significant at this size.
- **What it changes.** It changes the reading's shape: immediate, current-rich, a little less syzygetic.
