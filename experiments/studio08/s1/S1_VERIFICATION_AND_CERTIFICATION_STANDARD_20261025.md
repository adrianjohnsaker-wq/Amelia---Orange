## Scientific stop conditions (absolute)

**SC-1 Original science substrate absent.** The connected GitHub, Library and Dropbox searches as of this design found no exact Studio07/07b original runnable source or eight complete checkpoint pairs. Issue #7 is the source-copy admission request. The operator's written Studio07/07b aggregate report does not replace these bytes.

**SC-2 Fresh world construction unresolved.** Having 32 *different SHA seeds* does not prove 32 different initial full model states or genuinely independent synthetic worlds. World construction and historical-seed exclusion must be verified against original model, before S1 registration. If all 32 closed worlds are effectively identical, the registered 32-world independence claim fails.

**SC-3 Model-specific adapter not supplied.** The \`original_model_assay.mjs\` is an original-only harness contract. It MUST NOT be connected to toy houses or a synthesized replica of Studio07. A new adapter must expose original model state and exact causal cue hooks and demonstrate byte-for-byte baseline parity.

**SC-4 No independent seal.** Registered source/model/hook SHA256s, season/tick counts, 32-world starting state digests and original lesion timing must be locked and independently timestamped before the first science run. This candidate document contains unresolved parameters and is not yet sealed.

**SC-5 No source-choice observability or reward-safe trace.** Every raw transition must include exact prechoice/postchoice full-state digests and counterfactual score margins. The independently executed \`trace_integrity.py\` verifies bounded +0.35 amplitude and delayed reward conservation. Compromised paths invalidate that world and must be retained as protocol deviations, not silently replaced.

## Scientific claim test

An independently certified *policy advantage* on this artificial-life substrate would require verified C2–C5, positive adaptive-minus-round-robin paired effects on BOTH maintenance and repair, Holm-corrected positive results, no hidden world exclusions, and evidence that decisions changed causally because of acquired feedback rather than a fixed scheduling advantage.

The two current study arms isolate **adaptive policy** versus **deterministic fixed schedule**, not perfectly the contribution of reward learning alone; a further frozen-credit/sham-feedback arm may be needed to specifically attribute superiority to reward information. This is a prospective critique, not a hidden negative result.

Even a future positive S1 result supports adaptive use of provided receptors, **not the self-invention of receptors**, de novo genesis, general intelligence or intrinsic agency in an external beacon.
