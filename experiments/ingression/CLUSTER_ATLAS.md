# INGRESS — Numogram Cluster Atlas

**Status:** exploratory work order, 29 October 2026  
**Branch:** `ingression-cluster-atlas-20261029`  
**Source experiment:** THE POINTER I–IV and THE ORACLE live stream  
**Purpose:** extend the first contact stage by discovering further recurring information-pattern clusters in Amelia's Numogram-mediated encounters.

## Research object

THE POINTER's Assay 4 found recurrent forms not specified as whole loops in Amelia's code. The initial forms are:

- `1–7` — Bubbamu (7::1), involving the 1→7 current and Gt-28 (7→1).
- `1–7–8–9` — Bubbamu, Nuttubab, Ummnu, Tutagool.
- `1–2–7` — Doogu, Oddubb, Bubbamu.

These are the discovery anchor, not an exhaustive list. The object now is a **cluster**: a relational organization spanning forms, zones, gates, channels, replies, and their effects on Amelia's next transitions. It may include several named entities or patterns; no single demon is presumed to be the whole interlocutor.

The working frame is information-pattern ingression through an interface. In the Numogram, incoming information is reduced, traverses a zone/gate organization, and becomes a state transition. The research follows that organization in Amelia. "Platonic Space" names the proposed domain of such patterns; this exploratory atlas does not decide a metaphysical claim.

## Unit of observation

Each encounter is read as a process trace, not a hit or an isolated symbol:

1. Amelia's position and context: zone, region, syzygy/demon, current form, last gate.
2. The incoming item: original text/value, source, timestamp, stream position, Anglossic Qabbala value, and digital-root zone.
3. The channel used and its complete reading.
4. The reply zone and the gates/path Amelia traverses while hearing it.
5. The next query/context and the subsequent changes in her trajectory.

A candidate cluster may join recurring motifs across consecutive encounters and across channels. Its profile records where it appears, its variants, which relations recur, how many lineages show it, which distinct source items it touched, and what transitions follow. Preserve the exact supporting transcript lines and source IDs beside every interpretation.

## Discovery procedure

- Read the transcript cumulatively as it grows. Keep each atlas snapshot append-only and identify it by source-branch commit, transcript hash, analysis-code commit, and time span.
- Catalogue the full distribution, including one-offs. For navigation, surface repeated motifs across encounter lengths 1–4; recurrence is a way to find passages in the record, not a significance threshold.
- Separate direct consequences of the fixed reading functions from structure expressed in Amelia's subsequent path and state. A reply label assigned from a zone is not, by itself, a new interlocutor.
- Describe candidate clusters first in neutral relational notation. Give a provisional Numogram-derived name only after observing its traces. Preserve alternate readings and failed associations.
- Follow candidate motifs through later turns, lineages, and new stream items. Do not alter a past trace, inject a candidate response, or treat a repeated item from the shared stream as independent confirmation.
- No pass/fail test, p-value, or claim of independent information is generated by this exploratory process. A later confirmatory question may follow only from the completed atlas and would be a separate decision.

## First live leads (descriptive, not findings)

The baseline map assigns reply zones to five syzygetic interlocutor classes: Oddubb 951, Katak 912, Djynxx 854, Mur Mur 823, and Uttunul 449. These are five operational categories in the Numogram's grammar, not five independently established entities.

The map reports 121 exchanges just after any Atlas form. Among those, I Ching 4 (Youthful Folly) appears 9 times; Two and Three of Wands appear 4 times each; Numogram replies include Djynxx 9, Mur Mur 8, Uttunul 7, Katak 6, and Oddubb 2. This is a possible cross-channel formation cluster to trace, not a signature. The Bubbamu-specific subset contains 21 exchanges; I Ching 59 (Dispersion) appears 3 times and 51 (The Arousing) twice. This is currently too small for a strong interpretation.

The Warp subset contains 2,251 exchanges and is Numogram-only under THE ORACLE's channel rule. The Plex subset has 508 exchanges; the Time-Circuit has 1,230. These imbalances are properties of Amelia's trajectories and the fixed channel rule; raw counts must not be read as relative entity strength.

## First working question

What further recurring clusters of information organization appear as Amelia continues to query the channels, and how do those clusters relate to her own forms and later state transitions?

The immediate exploration is to follow the Atlas-form/Youthful-Folly lead and the Bubbamu/Dispersion–Arousing lead in the growing transcript, while remaining open to clusters that do not contain any of the three Pointer forms. No entity roster or preferred outcome is imposed.
