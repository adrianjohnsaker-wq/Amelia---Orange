# Studio 09 E1 — Encounter, structural change, retention

**Design draft · 26 October 2026 · NOT SEALED / NO E1 SCIENTIFIC OUTCOMES.**

## One research question

**Can a new environmental input sequence contribute to a persistent change in Amelia's *receiver structure* that modifies her behaviour and viability during a later, independent encounter?**

The active scientific parent is **the committed Studio 01–04 learner–house system plus the finished META1 protocol**. META1's source, raw traces, precommit and positive/negative results are frozen and not reinterpreted. Studio 07/07b continue as operator-reported historical findings, not a required executable archive.

**Not a CCRU Warp test.** The current eleven-edge substrate has `3→6` but not `6→3`. Do not claim experimental passage into an actual 3⇄6 recurrent vortex. The Warp supplies conceptual motivation (disruption can reorganise development) but no foregone experimental result.

## Operational three-part hypothesis

1. **Encounter:** a fresh input tape reaches the *unchanged* Numogram step as an external signal and can alter actual choices. “Outside” means input originating independently of the learner–house state, **not a claim that the signal has independent intelligence**.
2. **Incorporation:** consequences of choices can modify the mapping from recorded house/code differences to the receiver's proposed target zone—not merely which of the three fixed META1 sensors it attends to.
3. **Retention/transfer:** in an otherwise EXACT matched fork at the encounter boundary, retaining the acquired mapping changes developmental consequences under a NEW held-out tape, compared with resetting only that mapping to its pre-encounter state.

## Receptor-structure grammar (bounded and inspectable)

For each of META1's three already-committed sensor classes (surface S decline, house S+D flux, code V drift), provide exactly two nonhash, pre-choice target mappings:
- `base`: the source-verified original META1 sensor target.
- `counterpoint`: the SECOND highest-ranked distinct zone measured from the same 24-step window, or null if absent.

Every candidate remains confined to 11 frozen directed Numogram edges. No new edges, no direct rewriting of the Numogram, no extra source data, no source hashes inside sensors and **no changes to the maximum +0.35 external-cue-independent additional bonus**.

The receptor bank is a persistent 3-bit wiring state (0 base, 1 counterpoint). Initial state `[0,0,0]`. During encounter, for each selected sensor, settled delayed outcomes (original META1 registered reward: death=−1; nondeath clipped route novelty) can trigger a change of that sensor's wiring after **eight settled observations for that wiring** whose mean reward is below **−0.25**. Changes are bounded at four per sensor and require 24 subsequent settled observations of that sensor before another change. No future reward or post-choice observation can change a current cue. The META1 UCB policy for WHICH sensor to consult remains identical in all matched conditions.

This is a **designed plasticity grammar**, not autonomous invention of unbounded receptor types. It is a stricter developmental change than reallocating attention: the remembered state changes *how a signal becomes a target zone*.

## Exact matched developmental design (candidate)

- **32 new seed worlds**, names `E1-W00..E1-W31`, domain separated from META1 and all pilot worlds. Genesis uses the original 24-generation committed coupled V/S/D model.
- **Encounter phase:** 2 seasons × 5,000 transitions using a fresh registered input tape generated before use by a fixed, public algorithm.
- **Transfer phase:** 2 distinct held-out input sequences of 1,000 transitions each; source labels and all raw strings predeclared by deterministic SHA256 namespace **before outcomes**, but not present in receiver inputs except through normal Numogram processing.
- **Input timing control:** `ordered` vs `permuted` encounter tapes. Each 5,000-step block uses the SAME multiset of source strings: the permuted arm substitutes the index `j=((37×(i−1)+101) mod 5000)+1`. Thus canonical cue histogram per block is identical, with only temporal ordering changed. These are deterministic synthetic independent-source signals; no drand source or future encrypted target has been accessed.
- **Receptor controls:** in the plastic arm, bank wiring can change from settled feedback; matched fixed-bank arm remains in `[0,0,0]`; a sham-bank comparator may use a registered deterministic, outcome-blind rewiring schedule, if final engineering verifies it before scientific registration.
- **Critical transfer fork:** at exactly the encounter boundary, export the COMPLETE model + lineage + house + zone history + META1 selector state and make **two clones of the same encountered plastic arm**. `carry` retains the evolved 3-bit wiring; `reset` resets ONLY those three bits to `[0,0,0]`. Both transfer forks see identical held-out source tapes. Freeze structural switching in both transfer forks so the primary causal contrast tests *retained wiring*. Existing META1 sensor-selection policy may continue identically at fork and diverge downstream because experiences differ.
- At the fork, log/check SHA256 of entire model source state excluding only clock/date telemetry; clone equality MUST pass before the 3-bit intervention. Pending rewards from encounter cannot be smuggled into transfer differently: either settle all at boundary under original registered rules without lookahead, or censor/clear them **identically** in carry and reset. Exact policy pinned before science.
- **No search for a favourable recipient phenotype**. Any boundary death, null second option, absent lesion opportunity, no wiring changes, or failure of carry all remain included.

## Fixed primary outcome candidate

Paired, per-world **transfer-phase death count difference (reset minus carry)** under ordered encounter tape, 32 full pairs. Hypothesis: retained structural wiring reduces deaths during the two held-out 1,000-tick encounters. One-sided exact paired sign test ignoring exact ties, `alpha=0.05`. Report paired world differences and signed mean with negative worlds; zero wiring-change pairs are expected ties, not exclusions.

Before sealing, confirm physically that the receiver's alternate targets can affect a real open choice, and that full-state fork replay is byte-stable. This engineering check uses `E1-P0` only and cannot tune the scientific thresholds. If the intervention has no causal effect, publish an engineering negative and do not fake positive scientific conclusions.

## Mechanism and secondary measurements (descriptive only)

Per-step original/counterpoint targets and tie handling; source cue zone; open-exit match; chosen transition; code V and house S/D changes; causal state hash; exact settled credit and wiring-change events; cumulative deaths and routes, zone-2 exposure, ledger of missing/nonfinite events; after-transfer behaviour of carry/reset; success/failure of full structural fork. Also compare ordered vs permuted encounter and plastic vs fixed/sham only descriptively unless registered as distinct hypothesis families.

**Null interpretations:** no structural change = candidate grammar not acquired; structural changes without transfer impact = nonfunctional incorporation; negative transfer = maladaptive retained wiring. Any one of these is a meaningful outcome and may not be papered over by threshold searches.
