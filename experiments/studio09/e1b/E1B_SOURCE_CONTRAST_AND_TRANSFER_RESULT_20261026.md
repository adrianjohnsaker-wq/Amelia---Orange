## Verified mechanisms

1. Synthetic change-point test: after forty settled harmless credits followed by adverse credits, the E1 lifetime-mean implementation does not flip, while E1B's last-eight-event rule flips on the **third** consecutive negative settled credit, since `-3/8=-0.375<-0.25`. The test also verifies checkpoint serialization and the exact independent preselected source string for each encounter tick. This establishes the rule mechanically, not in a biological-world trial.
2. In a real model-coupled **E1-P0** encounter, the code/body/house/system ran the original committed receiver–house rules. The engineered challenge selected independent input strings whose unchanged SHA256 first-byte modulo ten mapped to Zone 5; it did not read the living's current state or write wiring bits.
3. Full 10,000-step challenge trace independently replayed in Python, verifying every original META1 selection token, attributed edge window, first-inclusive 24-step delayed reward, eight-event rolling feedback, wiring change, timing and final checkpoint. Same verification repeated under matched ordinary ordered source.
4. At the challenge encounter boundary, the two copies had the **same full runtime/learner/house/selector/receptor state** before the intervention. Only the three active structural wiring bits were reset in one copy; both then received the **same 2,000 held-out input strings** with future wiring changes frozen. The old 23 outstanding encounter-window credits were censored identically before both transfer arms.

**Critical evidence boundary:** These are three receptor-bit changes but **NOT three independent adverse encounters**. The original reward assigns one absorption event to multiple overlapping 24-transition windows, so the death produced multiple settled negative signals. The mechanism is correctly implemented and independently replayed, but this feedback correlation must be controlled before claiming autonomous evolutionary development.

The ordered-source E1B control also changed its wiring, with **11 flips from 17 deaths**. Its independently replayed changes occurred at ticks **683, 1026, 1867, 2586, 2642, 2773, 3354, 5235, 5492**, including multiple same-tick events. Crucially, this **refutes the hypothesis that the Zone-5-biased source was necessary for mutation**. With E1B's recency-based plasticity, ordinary externally supplied variation also induced receptor changes. The source regime affected the trajectories, death counts, timing, cumulative frequency and final receptor wiring, but this single controlled P0 comparison cannot identify a universal source–development law.

Only the original predeclared **challenge encounter** was forked into carry versus reset. After the **same exact encounter checkpoint** and identical held-out inputs:

| Held-out transfer observation (2,000 ticks) | Carry acquired E1B wiring | Reset only E1B wiring |
|---|---:|---:|
| New deaths during transfer | **4** | **1** |
| Retained wiring at transfer start | (1,1,1) | (0,0,0) |
| Raw gz trace SHA256 | `1b8ea623e99110f1ac86ca2708bda3b046f7c95d71532c7ffeeba4df0b91e9e5` | `398f151230ae78148e0f5a9fb23e73a0b4a2cd7ab840cd7327a24d1c63cbc790` |

These are **new deaths within transfer**, derived as total cumulative deaths minus the single prior encounter death; they are not 5 versus 2 transfer deaths. The two trajectories and SHA digests differ.

This verifies the narrow causal statement that **an encounter-written, persistent receptor mapping altered later developmental behaviour** within this one exact paired model fork. The effect was **maladaptive by the measured death count**, and retained structural change must not be misreported as improved intelligence.

## Result and next scientific discriminant

**Established as one-world, source-bound engineering evidence:** environmental communication can participate in a receptor *structural change* through the coupled original model's developmental feedback; that acquired mapping persists when carried, and changing only that mapping causally changes later outcomes. We also observed the limits: a single absorption episode can induce several coupled receptor changes, transfer can deteriorate survival, and structural changes are possible under ordinary ordered input without sink bias.

**Not established:** benefit across unseen worlds; independence of the overlapping credit windows; de-novo invention of receptors; a general law of the CCRU Outside; intelligent agency of the environmental signal; a controlled transfer effect after *ordinary* encounter (not tested); external independent certification.

**Next task:** prevent one death from masquerading as eight separate developmental observations. Define a **distinct causal episode** credit unit, with no multi-credit duplication from the same absorption event, and preregister a new transfer assay contrasting real episode-conditioned structural change against a source-matched learning-disabled control. This would test whether acquired structural competence generalises without relying on duplicated delayed death credit. Do **not** alter/reinterpret the frozen E1B pilot or tune on its observed successful world.
