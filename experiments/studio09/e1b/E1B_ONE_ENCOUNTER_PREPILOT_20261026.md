## Single problem being investigated

The earlier implementation accumulated **every** settled reward since birth of the receptor. The proposed rule was intended to make plasticity responsive to consequential new encounters, but a large harmless history can dilute later negative experience indefinitely. That creates a structural inertia beyond the stated threshold and cooldown.

**E1B changes exactly one aspect of structural learning:** each sensor's condition is evaluated against the **last eight settled rewards attributed to the CURRENT wiring**, not the lifetime mean. The numerical trigger remains mean strictly less than **−0.25**; the four-switch cap, twenty-four-settled-event cooldown, three original sensor definitions, transition graph, +0.35 cue and META1 UCB choice are unchanged. Late credits associated with an obsolete prior wiring are still excluded. This is a NEW variation defined prior to executing its real-model pilot, not a claim that E1's old behaviour was retrospectively incorrect.
