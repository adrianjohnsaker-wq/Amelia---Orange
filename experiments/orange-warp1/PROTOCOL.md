# ORANGE-WARP-1: blinded target-correspondence study

**Protocol frozen:** before target generation. **Repository source:** Amelia---Orange \`main\` at \`0c4b7c63b29a13eea3d556b5e02aa7bd87a545f3\`. This experiment uses the fixed Numogram core without editing \`src/\`.

## Claim and scope

Primary claim under test: a response generated by Amelia's full Numogram runtime after a standard 30-step encounter corresponds to a concealed, independently selected six-bit target more often when the two Time-Circuit-to-Warp ingress gates are enabled than when they are disabled.

The measured outcome is exact hexagram correspondence. This is an operational test of Warp-associated anomalous target correspondence. A positive result alone would not establish that the target is the metaphysical Outside, identify an entity, or validate any particular CCRU interpretation. A valid null is a negative result for this operational claim in this apparatus and protocol.

## Frozen response map

Use the six zone coordinates already used for Numogram line encoding, in bottom-to-top order: \`[1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5]\`. For each zone, the response bit is \`1\` iff its final activation after step 30 is greater than or equal to that zone's activation in \`INITIAL_ZONES\`; otherwise it is \`0\`. The six bits in this order are the complete 0–63 response. This direct comparison-to-start rule is fixed before target generation; no reference run, fitted threshold, or target-informed choice is used. Ties map to \`1\`. No alternative mapping or partial match is scored.

## Conditions and trial procedure

- **N = 512 paired trials**, each with one target-independent 30-token input tape. Tokens are 128-bit CSPRNG draws generated and hashed before target generation; the same ordered tape is used in both members of its pair.
- **Warp-on:** the full 29-edge \`CANONICAL_GATES\` graph from the pinned source.
- **Warp-off:** the same graph with only the two ingress edges \`2→3\` and \`5→6\` removed. All other edges, including internal Warp edges, are unchanged.
- Each member starts from a newly constructed runtime at Zone 0. Run exactly 30 calls to \`step(token)\` and record every token, transition, zone activation vector, and memory-head digest. Randomize order within pair using an independent CSPRNG coin. No target, target label, target-derived value, or feedback is passed to Amelia or the runner.
- Before the first runtime call, an independent target-generation process draws 512 independent uniform integers in \`[0,63]\`, encrypts them with AES-256-GCM, writes the key outside the runner's directory, and publishes the ciphertext hash and plaintext commitment. The runner has no path or key for target material. It writes all outputs and a completion digest. Only after that digest is fixed may a separate scoring process decrypt targets and join by trial ID.

## Primary analysis and sample size

For each pair define a Warp-on-only hit (\`b\`) and Warp-off-only hit (\`c\`). The primary test is the one-sided exact McNemar test: conditional on \`b+c\`, evaluate \`b\` under \`Binomial(b+c, 0.5)\`, alternative \`p>0\`, α = .05.

The joint decision also requires Warp-on's hit count to exceed the exact one-sided binomial chance null \`p=1/64\` at α=.05. Both gates must pass; each is reported with its exact p-value and confidence interval. No interim analysis, optional stopping, exclusions after target reveal, or secondary scoring.

**Power justification:** the smallest effect treated as scientifically meaningful for planning is \`p_on=.05\` against \`p_off=1/64\`. At N=512, exact binomial power for the Warp-on chance-reference test is 0.996. Exact McNemar power is evaluated across the full feasible joint-hit range (all \`p11\` from 0 to \`1/64\`) while holding those marginals fixed; its minimum is 0.9113. By the union bound, power for the conjunction of both decision gates is at least 0.907. This power statement is conditional on those planning marginals; smaller effects may be missed. N is fixed at 512 and will not be extended after seeing results.
